## How We Compare Shanghai to USA Ocean Freight Providers

Side-by-side comparisons of Shanghai-to-USA ocean freight quotes only work if you’re measuring the same services across providers. A low-looking rate that excludes customs brokerage or last-mile delivery isn’t actually cheaper once those costs appear later. These four factors help distinguish a complete quote from a partial one.

### Door-to-Door Service and Last-Mile Delivery

“Door-to-door” should cover the trip from factory pickup through final delivery, not just port-to-port service that leaves delivery up to you. Confirm whether the quote includes inland trucking in China, ocean freight, US customs clearance, and final delivery to your warehouse, store, or Amazon FBA facility.

Some forwarders list these services separately; others, like [AEB Logistics](https://aeblogistics.com/ocean-freight-china-to-usa/), include pickup, customs coordination, and delivery in one proposal. If the delivery location has no loading dock, ask whether liftgate service is included or billed separately. It’s commonly an added accessorial charge.

### Customs Support and Importer Responsibilities

Importers remain legally responsible for duties and taxes, no matter who files the paperwork. A forwarder can coordinate customs brokerage, offer HS/HTS code guidance, and explain bond options, but that doesn’t change who owes the US government money. It changes how much manual filing work you handle yourself.

For Amazon FBA shipments, Amazon does not act as Importer of Record and will refuse shipments with unpaid duties or shipping charges. Before cargo leaves Shanghai, confirm in writing who will file the entry and who will fund the duty payment.

### Quote Transparency and Total Landed Cost

A complete quote separates freight costs from destination charges, customs brokerage fees, and accessorial charges such as residential delivery or liftgate service. Ask each forwarder for an itemized breakdown rather than a single lump sum. Then compare quotes using the same Incoterm (FOB, EXW, or DDP), so you’re not comparing a port-to-port rate with a full door-to-door rate. Reviewing a [freight forwarder comparison for China to USA imports](https://aeblogistics.com/freight-forwarder-comparison-for-china-to-usa-imports/) side by side makes it easier to spot gaps before booking.

### Tracking, Communication, and Importer Fit

Shipment visibility matters once cargo leaves Shanghai, especially at milestones like export customs, departure, and import arrival. Some forwarders offer live dashboard tracking, while others rely on email updates from an account manager. Match the communication style to your operation: a dedicated contact often suits smaller importers managing one or two shipments at a time. Larger programs with constant container movement may prefer a digital platform.

## How to Choose and Compare Quotes

Getting quotes you can actually compare takes more upfront effort than asking each forwarder for a single number. Before you compare any figures, settle the questions below.

### Should You Request LCL or FCL Pricing?

Request both if your shipment is near the breakeven point, usually around 10 to 15 cubic meters. The cheaper option depends on your cargo’s exact volume and weight. LCL combines your freight with other shippers’ cargo, adding handling points and potentially extending transit time. FCL gives you exclusive use of a container and simplifies customs because your cargo isn’t mixed with other shipments. A [shanghai freight forwarder](https://aeblogistics.com/shanghai-freight-forwarder/) that quotes both options side by side can show you the real cost difference for your cargo, rather than leaving you to guess.

### Which Origin, Cargo, and Delivery Details Should You Provide?

Give every forwarder the same shipment details so you can compare quotes: origin and destination, Incoterms (FOB or EXW), HS code, cargo value, box count, cubic meters, weight in kilograms, and estimated cargo-ready date. Providing an accurate commercial invoice and packing list early prevents misunderstandings. Accurate product descriptions also help prevent classification disputes when your cargo reaches US customs.

### Which Destination Charges and Customs Costs Are Included?

Ask whether the quote includes US destination handling, customs brokerage, duty and tax estimates, and inland delivery, or whether the forwarder bills for any of these separately after arrival. Customs brokerage often costs around USD 125, depending on the number of commodities in the shipment. So a quote that leaves out this line item isn’t necessarily cheaper; it may just be incomplete. Confirm whether you need a customs bond and who will arrange it.

### Who Handles Delivery Appointments and Accessorial Fees?

For Amazon FBA shipments, confirm who schedules the delivery appointment through Amazon’s Carrier Appointment Request Portal and what documents they need from you, including the bill of lading, FBA booking form, and carrier manual. For non-FBA deliveries, ask about accessorial fees such as liftgate service, often listed at around USD 100 for locations without a loading dock. Check for extra charges for residential delivery addresses, too.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which Shanghai-to-USA ocean freight company is best for a small importer?

Small importers and Amazon FBA sellers generally do best with a China-specialist forwarder that offers one account manager, consolidated LCL options, and built-in FBA delivery scheduling. Global carriers and enterprise forwarders often set higher minimum volume expectations. Compare at least two or three quotes with identical Incoterms and cargo details before deciding.

### How much does ocean freight from Shanghai to the USA cost?

Ocean freight rates change frequently with container type, season, and carrier capacity, so confirm costs with a shipment-specific quote. Customs brokerage, if priced separately, commonly costs around USD 125, depending on the number of commodities in the shipment.

### How long does sea freight from Shanghai to the USA take?

Door-to-door ocean freight from China generally takes about 30 to 40 days. Actual transit times depend on customs processing, port congestion, carrier schedules, and seasonal demand.

Express courier and air freight options are faster, but cost considerably more per kilogram. While FedEx and other express shipping services deliver urgent packages in days, ocean freight remains the most cost-effective solution for bulk commercial inventory.

### Is LCL or FCL better for shipping from Shanghai?

FCL tends to suit larger shipments that need dedicated container space and a lower per-unit cost, while LCL works for smaller shipments that don’t justify a full container. The better option depends on your cargo volume, so it’s worth requesting prices for both before deciding.

### Does a door-to-door ocean freight quote include customs duties and taxes?

Not automatically. Importers remain responsible for duties and taxes, even when a forwarder coordinates customs clearance. Amazon also won’t accept FBA shipments with unpaid duties or shipping charges. Confirm in writing whether your quote includes duty and tax payments or bills for them separately.

### What should I ask a freight forwarder before booking a Shanghai-to-USA shipment?

Ask for an itemized quote that covers freight, destination handling, customs brokerage, and extra fees like liftgate service or residential delivery. Confirm who files the customs entry, whether you need a Power of Attorney, and who schedules delivery appointments if your shipment is going to an Amazon FBA facility.
