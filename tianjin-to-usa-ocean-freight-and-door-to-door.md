## Choose Your Ocean Shipment and Delivery Scope

 First, decide how much of the ocean freight and delivery chain you want us to coordinate. Tianjin shippers generally choose between a dedicated container (FCL) and shared container space (LCL), then decide how far delivery should extend beyond the U.S. port.

### FCL Shipping for Dedicated Container Space

 Full container load shipping makes sense when your cargo fills, or nearly fills, a 20-foot, 40-foot, or 40-foot high-cube container. You get the whole box, so nothing else touches your goods between Tianjin Xingang Port and the U.S. destination.

 FCL often suits larger orders, fragile or high-value cargo, and importers looking for lower per-unit shipping costs. We coordinate factory pickup, container booking, customs clearance paperwork, and door-to-door delivery as one package. Cargo insurance is also available for added protection.

### LCL Shipping for Smaller Loads and Multiple Suppliers

 Less than container load shipping works for cargo that doesn’t justify a full container. We consolidate your shipment with other cargo heading to the same destination, so you pay for the space you use rather than an entire box.

 This option suits smaller Amazon FBA restock orders and buyers sourcing from multiple Tianjin-area suppliers who want to combine everything into one shipment. Consolidation adds handling time at both ends, but that can be a fair trade for lower shipping costs on smaller volumes.

## How Much Does Tianjin-to-USA Shipping Cost?

 Ocean freight rates from Tianjin depend on container size, destination port, and how much of the door-to-door shipping chain you include in your quote. End-to-end door-to-door shipping simplifies budgeting by consolidating origin trucking, sea transit, and final delivery into a single workflow.

 A 20-foot or 40-foot FCL shipment to the U.S. West Coast typically costs less than the same container routed to the East Coast. East Coast routes take longer and may include a Panama Canal transshipment leg. Overall pricing for China to USA freight also shifts with the season. Peak season surcharges apply during high-demand periods, while volume discounts may be available for full container load shipments.

### What an Itemized Door-to-Door Quote Should Cover

 A complete door-to-door quote should list factory pickup, export documentation, ocean freight, U.S. import clearance, and final delivery as separate line items. We can provide an itemized breakdown on request, so you can see what each stage costs rather than just one bundled figure.

 We can include customs brokerage in your shipment quote. It typically costs around USD 125, depending on how many commodities appear on the entry.

### How to Compare FCL, LCL, Duties, and Total Landed Cost

 Freight rates aren’t the same as your landed cost. Landed cost includes import duties, Section 301 tariffs where applicable, customs brokerage, and inland delivery charges on top of the ocean rate.

 Two quotes with similar freight rates can lead to very different landed costs once you account for duty rates and HS code classifications. Confirm your product’s HTS code before comparing quotes, since that code affects the duty calculation more than the shipping mode does.

## What Does U.S. Customs Clearance Require?

 Smooth US customs clearance requires accurate documentation before your cargo arrives, not after. Missing paperwork or an unclear Importer of Record can hold a container at the port, even when the vessel arrives on schedule.

### Documents and Filing Responsibilities for Ocean Imports

 Every ocean shipment needs a commercial invoice, packing list, and bill of lading, and may also need a certificate of origin. For applicable ocean cargo, Importer Security Filing (ISF) data is generally due no later than 24 hours before the cargo loads onto the vessel in Tianjin.

 CBP Form 7501 (entry summary) reports the classification, value, and estimated duties once the shipment reaches the U.S. The importer of record remains responsible for the accuracy of this information, even when a licensed customs broker files it on their behalf.

### HTS Classification, Duties, and Section 301 Tariffs

 Your product’s Harmonized Tariff Schedule (HTS) code determines the applicable duty rate. Section 301 tariffs may apply on top of the standard rate for certain product categories from China. Make sure the classification matches the product’s actual material, function, and use, not just a code your supplier suggests.

 We can help with HS/HTS code guidance and estimate import duties and taxes based on your shipment details, so you can see an estimated landed cost before committing to the shipment.

### Customs Bonds, Broker Coordination, and Importer Obligations

 Commercial imports valued above USD 2,500 generally require a customs bond. We can arrange an annual bond for U.S. imports or offer the use of an AEB bond on a case-by-case basis. We also require a Power of Attorney when you ask us to handle customs clearance on your behalf.

 Importers remain responsible for duties and taxes owed to CBP. Amazon doesn’t act as Importer of Record, and FBA shipments with unpaid duties, taxes, or shipping charges won’t be accepted.

Tianjin shipments to the West Coast often clear faster than East Coast shipments routed through the Panama Canal. Actual timing depends on customs processing, port congestion, and carrier schedules, so treat published ranges as planning estimates, not guaranteed dates.

### How much does door-to-door ocean freight from Tianjin cost?

### Should I choose FCL or LCL for my shipment from Tianjin?

 FCL suits cargo that fills most of a container. It generally lowers your per-unit shipping cost and avoids extra consolidation handling. LCL works well for smaller shipments or orders combined from several Tianjin-area suppliers, though it adds a few handling days at origin and destination.

### Are U.S. customs duties included in a door-to-door quote?

 Duties and taxes are the importer’s responsibility, and we typically estimate them separately from the freight rate. They aren’t automatically included in every door-to-door quote.
