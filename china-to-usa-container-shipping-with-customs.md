### How Do 20-Foot, 40-Foot, and Shared-Container Costs Compare?

The more of a container you use, the lower your cost per unit. Full container load (FCL) gives you a dedicated 20-foot, 40-foot, or 40-foot high-cube container. It suits larger orders and can lower your per-unit shipping cost. Less than container load (LCL) puts your cargo in a shared container, so you pay for the space it uses.

Both weight and volume matter. Ocean pricing for shared cargo is based on cubic meters (CBM), while full containers are priced per box. We quote the option that fits your CBM, weight, and cargo-ready date. If your volume is close to the cutoff, we can price a 20-foot and 40-foot container side by side.

### Which Charges Affect Your Total Landed Cost?

Total landed cost combines the product price, freight, destination charges, duties and taxes, and delivery, expressed per unit. Skip this math, and your margins can take a hit after the goods arrive.

Charges that can change the total include:

- Origin charges in China, such as export customs, loading, and documentation
- Destination charges, such as unloading and terminal handling
- Customs brokerage and bond costs
- Duties, tariffs, and fees on your HTS code
- Cargo insurance, if you request it
- Demurrage and detention if a container is held or returned late
- Inland delivery, including liftgate or residential fees

### How Do EXW, FOB, CIF, and DDP Change Your Responsibilities?

Your Incoterm determines who pays for and arranges each leg. Under EXW, you take responsibility from the supplier’s door, including export clearance in China. Under FOB, the seller handles the goods through loading onto the vessel, and you take over from there. Under CIF, the seller pays freight and insurance to the destination port, while you handle import clearance and delivery.

DDP shipping differs from ordinary door-to-door service. With DDP, duties and taxes are settled before the cargo reaches your address, so you don’t receive a separate bill at delivery. Door-to-door service can still leave duties to you. Importers remain legally responsible for duties and taxes whichever term they use, so confirm in writing what a DDP quote covers. ## Choose the Right Container and Delivery Plan

Choose FCL when your cargo fills most of a container, and LCL when it doesn’t. Ocean freight is the standard route for larger or heavier shipments from China to the USA. The right plan also depends on your origin port, US gateway, and delivery address.

### FCL Shipping with 20-Foot, 40-Foot, and High-Cube Containers

FCL gives you a dedicated container that stays sealed from pickup through delivery, which means fewer handling touchpoints. We book 20-foot, 40-foot, and 40-foot high-cube containers.

A 20-foot box suits dense, heavy goods such as machinery or tile. A 40-foot box works well for general cargo like furniture and consumer goods. A high-cube container adds height for light, bulky products such as packaging or foam. Check weight as well as volume: an overloaded container brings fines and safety risks.

We coordinate FCL services that include factory pickup, customs clearance, cargo insurance coordination, multi-supplier consolidation where applicable, and Amazon FBA forwarding.

LCL works for importers who can’t fill a container. We consolidate smaller shipments into shared containers. The service includes door-to-door transportation, customs clearance coordination, warehousing and distribution, and delivery to Amazon FBA.

Buying from several Chinese factories? We can combine cargo from multiple suppliers into one shipment. That means one booking, one set of export paperwork, and one arrival to plan around.

## Customs Clearance and Import Responsibilities

You’re responsible for duties, taxes, and accurate filings on every container, whoever prepares the paperwork. A freight forwarder coordinates the process. The details below show who does what. Tariff rates and rules change, so verify current requirements with us and US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) before you ship.

### Who Acts as Importer of Record and Customs Broker?

The importer of record is legally responsible for clearing the goods and paying duties. That’s usually the buyer. A customs broker files the entry with CBP on the importer’s behalf.

CBP doesn’t require you to hire a broker, but first-time importers and anyone shipping regulated goods can benefit from one. We coordinate customs brokerage and support you with customs documentation, so you’re not filing alone. Our [customs brokerage](https://aeblogistics.com/customs-brokerage/) service explains the scope.

### Commercial Invoice, Packing List, and Bill of Lading Requirements

Every container shipment requires essential import documents that must agree with each other. Mismatches in carton counts, weights, or product descriptions can trigger extra scrutiny at the port.

For air shipments, an air waybill replaces the ocean bill of lading, but ocean containers rely on three key records:

- **Commercial invoice:** seller and buyer details, itemized products, unit and total value, HTS codes, Incoterm, and country of origin
- **Packing list:** carton dimensions, weights, markings, and contents, without prices
- **Bill of Lading:** the carrier’s contract and receipt for the shipment, issued after booking
 

Ask your supplier for drafts before the cargo is ready. That way, you can fix errors while the goods are still in the factory.

### ISF Filing, Customs Bonds, and Power of Attorney

Ocean shipments need an Importer Security Filing (ISF, also called ISF 10+2) at least 24 hours before cargo loads onto a vessel bound for the United States. A compliant ISF 10+2 filing prevents steep CBP fines and customs holds, so submit details early.

A customs bond guarantees payment of duties and fees. For US imports, we can arrange an annual bond or, case by case, offer the use of an AEB bond. We need a Power of Attorney if you ask us to handle customs clearance. Our guide to [essential customs strategies for shipping from China to the USA](https://aeblogistics.com/shipping-from-china-to-usa-essential-customs-strategies/) covers this step in more detail.

### HTS Classification, Import Duties, and Product Compliance

Your HTS code sets your duty rate, so it’s worth getting the classification right before you place an order. A wrong code can lead to overpayment or a compliance flag at entry. We provide HS/HTS code guidance and documentation support, plus duty and tax estimates once we have your shipment details.

Costs to expect on a container include:

- Import duties under your HTS code
- Section 301 tariffs on many goods from China
- Anti-dumping duties or countervailing duties on certain targeted products
- The merchandise processing fee of 0.3464% of declared value
- The harbor maintenance fee of 0.125% of value on ocean shipments
 

Some products also need agency approval, such as FCC certification for electronics. Check product rules before you buy. ### Does door-to-door container shipping include customs duties and taxes?

Not automatically. Door-to-door service covers transport and customs coordination, but you’re responsible for duties and taxes unless the shipment uses DDP terms. Ask us to confirm in writing what’s included in your quote. Importers remain legally responsible for duties and taxes under any Incoterm.

### What is the current import duty on goods shipped from China to the USA?

The rate depends on your HTS code, product, and country of origin. Many goods from China also carry Section 301 tariffs on top of standard duty. Tariff policy changes, so we don’t publish a single figure here. Send us your HTS code and shipment details, and we’ll provide a duty estimate for you to verify against current CBP rules.

### Does US customs inspect every shipping container?

No. CBP selects some shipments for examination to check value, origin, labeling, and compliance. Intensive inspections are less common. Complete, matching paperwork and the correct HTS code can reduce the risk of holds, but we can’t guarantee customs release.

### Is FCL or LCL cheaper for my shipment from China?

LCL usually costs less overall for small volumes, while FCL costs less per unit as you fill a container when shipping from China to USA. If your cargo is close to a full 20-foot container, ask us to price both options side by side. Your CBM, weight, and delivery timing will help determine which offers better value.

### Can AEB Logistics deliver a cleared container shipment to Amazon FBA?

Yes. We support the entire process, from factory pickup to Amazon FBA delivery. That includes FBA labeling, palletization, appointment scheduling, and last-mile delivery by LTL or small parcel. Duties, taxes, and shipping charges must be paid before delivery, as Amazon won’t accept shipments with unpaid amounts.
