## What Does Door-to-Door Shipping Cost?

 Door-to-door shipping costs depend on shipment mode, cargo volume or weight, and whether duties are prepaid under Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) terms. Ocean and air shipping rates fluctuate based on seasonal capacity, but the cost components stay consistent: origin pickup, international freight, customs entry, duties and taxes, and final delivery.

 Budgeting accurately for China to USA shipping means evaluating all route variables ahead of time.

 Total landed cost is the number that matters for budgeting, not just the freight rate. It adds the product cost to freight charges, customs duties, insurance, and destination delivery, so you know the real cost per unit before the cargo lands.

### How Shipment Size, CBM, and Chargeable Weight Affect Freight Rates

 Ocean freight rates depend on cubic meters (CBM) for less than container load (LCL) shipments, or on container size for full container load (FCL). Air freight rates depend on chargeable weight, which is whichever is greater between actual weight and volumetric weight.

 Light, bulky cargo can cost more than its actual weight suggests once volumetric weight applies. Including accurate box counts, CBM, and kilograms in the quote request helps avoid that surprise later.

### DDP vs. Standard Import Terms: What Changes in the Total Landed Cost?

 Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) shipping means duties, taxes, and clearance fees go into one price, so there’s no separate duty invoice after delivery. Standard import terms, such as FOB or EXW, leave customs duties and import-entry costs with the importer, separate from the freight charge.

 Using DDP shipping simplifies budgeting for first-time importers and Amazon sellers who want one predictable number. Established importers with their own customs broker may prefer to see the duty line separately. Either way, importers remain responsible for duties and taxes owed to US Customs and Border Protection, whether those costs are prepaid in a DDP quote or billed afterward.

 Cargo insurance is also worth coordinating separately. It protects the declared value against loss or damage in transit and isn’t automatically part of every freight quote.

## Choose the Right Transit Option for Your Shipment

 The right transit option depends on how quickly inventory needs to arrive and how much it costs to hold cargo in transit versus paying a premium for speed. Air freight, ocean freight, and express courier each serve a different part of a replenishment strategy, and most importers use more than one depending on the shipment.

### Air Freight for Time-Sensitive Inventory and Replenishment

 Air freight from China suits urgent restocks, product launches, and higher-value goods when a few weeks of transit time is too slow. AEB Logistics coordinates air cargo with packing and labeling support, customs clearance, and door-to-door delivery, including direct delivery to Amazon FBA facilities.

 For example, air freight from Hong Kong to Los Angeles can take around five days. General Amazon FBA guidance puts standard air freight lead times closer to 7 to 10 days once export handling and destination trucking are included. Air freight is usually faster but more expensive than ocean freight, so it works best for smaller or time-critical shipments rather than routine bulk restocking.

### Ocean Freight: LCL or FCL for Cost-Efficient Volume

 Ocean freight is the cost-efficient choice for larger or heavier shipments that aren’t time-critical. Full container load (FCL) gives a shipment dedicated container space, available in 20-foot, 40-foot, and 40-foot high-cube options. It suits larger cargo volumes, added cargo security, or a lower per-unit shipping cost.

 Less than container load (LCL) sea freight consolidates smaller shipments into shared containers, so you pay only for the space your cargo uses. This works for businesses that don’t have enough volume to fill a container but still want ocean freight’s lower cost per unit.

 AEB Logistics also offers consolidated shipping for buyers combining cargo from multiple suppliers into one shipment. That’s common for importers sourcing from more than one factory in Ningbo, Qingdao, or Shanghai.

### Express Courier for Samples and Urgent Small Parcels

 Express shipping through carriers like DHL, UPS, and FedEx moves small parcels fastest, typically for product samples, replacement parts, or urgent top-up orders. It costs more per kilogram than standard air freight, but for shipments under roughly 150 kg, express courier delivery is often simpler than booking air freight consolidation.

### Typical China-to-USA Transit Times and What Can Affect Them

 Published general transit estimates for China-to-USA shipping run about 3 to 5 days for express courier, 8 to 10 days for standard air freight, and 30 to 40 days for ocean freight door to door. These are planning estimates, not guarantees.

 Actual transit times shift with customs processing, port congestion, weather, carrier schedules, and seasonal demand. Shipments moving through West Coast ports like Los Angeles or Long Beach often clear faster than those headed to East Coast destinations like New York or Savannah, simply because of sailing distance.

 Inland trucking time can offset some of that difference, with rail and road routes reaching distribution hubs like Chicago or southern centers near Houston. Air freight routed through hubs like LAX, ORD, JFK, or SFO also adds handling time on arrival before final delivery.

## Customs, Duties, and Amazon FBA Delivery Requirements

 US customs clearance is required for every China-to-USA shipment, and importers remain responsible for duties and taxes regardless of who files the entry. AEB Logistics coordinates customs brokerage and documentation, but the legal duty to pay what’s owed sits with the importer of record.

### How U.S. Customs Clearance and Import Entry Support Work

 Import customs clearance requires a commercial invoice, packing list, and bill of lading or air waybill submitted to US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) before cargo is released. AEB Logistics coordinates this process and can use an AEB bond for customs entry in applicable cases.

 Where relevant, the company can also arrange an annual bond for the importer. A Power of Attorney is required when AEB Logistics performs customs clearance on the importer’s behalf.

### HS Classification, Tariffs, Duties, and Taxes

 Every product needs an accurate HS/HTS code because that code determines the duty rate and whether additional tariffs apply. AEB Logistics provides HS/HTS code guidance and documentation support, along with import duty and tax estimates when full shipment information arrives upfront.

 Getting the classification right before the shipment leaves China helps avoid reclassification delays and unexpected duty bills at the US border. Importers should also account for customs entry user fees, including the merchandise processing fee where applicable.

### DDP Air and Sea Options: When They May Fit

 DDP air freight and DDP sea freight bundle duties and taxes into the freight quote, so there’s no separate customs bill after delivery. Choosing air freight DDP provides rapid clearance for high-priority inventory, while sea freight DDP delivers dependable savings on bulk container volumes.

 This structure suits importers who want one predictable landed cost, particularly new importers without an existing customs broker relationship.

 Not every shipment or product category qualifies for the same DDP structure, so confirm DDP eligibility and scope against the specific commodity and destination before booking.

 For sellers managing Amazon FBA shipping, strict compliance with intake guidelines is mandatory. Amazon doesn’t act as Importer of Record and won’t accept FBA shipments arriving with unpaid duties, taxes, or shipping charges.
