## Common Questions About AEB Logistics Shipping From China to the USA

### How long does ocean freight take from China to the United States?

Ocean freight usually takes 25 to 40 days from port to port. The actual time depends on your ports and the route.

Door-to-door service takes a bit longer. You’ll need to factor in inland transport and customs clearance. Most door-to-door shipments arrive in 30 to 50 days.

Main Chinese ports include Shanghai, Shenzhen, and Ningbo. In the US, [major destinations](https://aeblogistics.com/china-to-usa/) are Los Angeles, Long Beach, New York, and Savannah. [Shipping routes and ports](https://overseasprojectcargo.com/NewsAndBlogDetails/shipping-from-china-to-usa-ultimate-guide-456bf2056f/) can make a big difference in your transit time.

### What is the cost per kilogram to ship from China to the United States?

The cost per kilogram depends on your shipping method:

**Ocean Freight**

- [Less Than Container Load](https://aeblogistics.com/lcl-delivery/) (LCL): $2 to $8 per kg
- Full Container Load (FCL): $0.50 to $3 per kg for large volumes

**Air Freight**

- Standard air cargo: $4 to $12 per kg
- Express courier: $8 to $20 per kg

Final prices vary. Both weight and volume matter—carriers always charge for whichever is higher. The season matters too since prices jump during [peak periods](https://aeblogistics.com/lunar-new-year-shipping-from-china/) like pre-Chinese New Year and holiday seasons.

Fuel surcharges and security fees may get added on top. Sometimes these are included in quotes, other times they’re listed separately.

### What are typical air cargo delivery times from China to America?

Standard air freight takes 5 to 10 days airport-to-airport. That covers flight time and basic handling.

Express couriers like DHL, FedEx, and UPS deliver in [3 to 7 days](https://www.transifly.com/shipping-from-china/). Big cities usually get faster service.

Door-to-door air freight takes 7 to 14 days. That covers pickup, export clearance, the flight, import clearance, and final delivery.

### What import duties and taxes will I pay on shipments from China to America?

Importing from China means you’ll pay a few fees:

**Customs Duties**

- Rates from 0% to 37.5% depending on product type
- Tariffs based on the Harmonized Tariff Schedule (HTS) code
- Some items get hit with extra Section 301 tariffs

**Merchandise Processing Fee (MPF)**

- 0.3464% of shipment value
- Minimum: $27.75
- Maximum: $538.40

**Harbor Maintenance Fee (HMF)**

- 0.125% of cargo value
- Only for ocean freight

**Import Taxes**

- De minimis exemption for shipments under $800
- Packages below this value enter duty-free
- Most personal shipments get the tax exemption

Your total duty depends on your product. Clothing, electronics, and furniture all have different rates. Check [current tariff info](https://amzprep.com/shipping-china-to-usa/) for your products using the HTS code.

Some products qualify for reductions or exemptions under trade agreements. Ask your customs broker if you’re not sure—you might save more than you expect.
