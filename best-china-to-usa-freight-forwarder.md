### China to USA Shipping Costs: Air vs. Sea

Air freight costs a lot more than sea freight, but it’s much faster. Expect to pay around $3 to $8 per kilogram for air, while sea freight runs about $500 to $2,000 per container.

**Air Freight Benefits:**

- Delivery in just 3-7 days
- Great for urgent shipments
- Less risk of damage along the way
 
**Sea Freight Benefits:**

- Way cheaper per unit
- Best for big, heavy shipments
- Perfect for stuff that isn’t time-sensitive
 
If you’re shipping under 200 kilograms, air freight might be worth the price. For anything over 500 kilograms, [sea freight is usually your best bet](https://aeblogistics.com/freight-forwarder-from-china-to-usa/).

Final costs depend on the shipment’s weight, size, and where it’s going. Shipping to coastal US cities is typically less expensive than going inland.

### Customs Processing for China to USA Shipments: What Really Happens?

Shipping goods from China to the USA? Customs processing is a crucial step you just can’t skip. It’s where your shipment either glides through or hits a few bumps.

Your freight partner usually takes care of most of the customs clearance work. They handle the paperwork and keep things moving.

First off, they send your import documents to US Customs. You’ll need a commercial invoice, a packing list, and a bill of lading.

Customs officials then review all your shipment details. They look at the declared value, product descriptions, and the country where everything comes from.

**Required Documents for China to USA Shipments:**

- Commercial invoice with accurate values
- Packing list showing all items
- Import license (if your products need one)
- Certificate of origin
 
Sometimes, customs selects shipments for a physical inspection. This can tack on another 2-3 days, which is a hassle but honestly, it’s pretty common.

Your freight partner pays any duties and taxes for you. You’ll pay them back later, or sometimes you prepay these costs up front.

Most China to USA shipments clear customs in just a day or two. If your products are complicated or really valuable, it might take a bit longer.
