     ### What is included in a door-to-door or DDP quote?

A complete door-to-door quote should combine factory pickup in Qingdao, export paperwork, ocean or air freight, customs clearance coordination, and final delivery in one price. AEB Logistics structures its quotes this way. It also offers cargo insurance as an add-on and provides an itemized breakdown on request.

DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) goes a step further: the forwarder handles import duties and taxes upfront, so you get one landed cost. Not every forwarder offers DDP by default, and terms vary by cargo type and destination. Before treating a quote as final, confirm that it includes duty payment.

### Which charges could be added after the initial quote?

Base ocean freight rates rarely cover everything. Peak season surcharges can push up prices in Q3 and Q4, ahead of the winter retail rush. Destination handling, fuel surcharges, and customs brokerage fees often fall outside the headline rate, too.

For instance, a location without a loading dock may need liftgate service, which can add a fee (AEB Logistics states USD 100). Ask every forwarder to name accessorial charges individually, rather than grouping them under a lump “destination fees” line.

### Who handles US customs entry, duties, and the importer’s bond?

The importer of record remains responsible for duties and taxes, regardless of who files the entry paperwork. A forwarder can coordinate customs clearance and offer bond options, including an AEB bond that AEB Logistics uses case by case. But the importer still holds the legal responsibility.

You need a Power of Attorney whenever you ask a forwarder to file customs entry on your behalf. For Amazon FBA shipments, Amazon won’t act as importer of record and will reject cargo that arrives with unpaid duties or shipping charges.

Staying compliant with US import regulations is essential to avoid penalties and delays. This involves accurate tariff classification, meeting partner agency rules, and timely entry summary submissions.

### How will you track the shipment from pickup to delivery?

Ask for named tracking milestones, not just a vague promise of “real-time visibility.” AEB Logistics tracks shipments through stages such as Accepted, In Transit, Export Customs, Export Departure, Import Entry, Import Arrival, and Delivered. That way, you can see where a delay happened.

Find out whether tracking updates come through a dashboard, email, or only on request. It affects how quickly you’ll hear about a customs hold or missed vessel connection.

## What to Confirm Before Booking

### Does the forwarder handle your specific Qingdao origin and US delivery address?

Confirm pickup coverage for your exact factory city. Qingdao freight forwarders often serve Qingdao and nearby areas, including Weifang, Jinan, and Yantai, but not every forwarder reaches every inland supplier. On the US side, check whether the forwarder delivers to your actual warehouse or Amazon FBA center, or stops at a port and hands the shipment to a separate trucker.

### Should your cargo move by FCL, LCL, or air freight?

Cargo volume usually decides between full container load (FCL) and less than container load (LCL) consolidation. Shipments above roughly 15 CBM tend to cost less per unit with FCL shipping.

For smaller consignments, LCL shipping lets you pay only for the cubic meters you use. Choosing less than container load (LCL) consolidation keeps inventory moving without paying for unused container space.

Air freight works well for urgent or high-value cargo. An air shipment from Hong Kong to Los Angeles can take around five days, compared with at least three weeks for ocean freight on the same lane. General published FBA guidance estimates air freight at roughly 7 to 10 days, depending on routing.

### What documents and cargo details are needed for an accurate quote?

To get an accurate quote, provide complete shipping documentation and shipment specifications. You will need the origin and destination, Incoterms (FOB or EXW), HS code, cargo value, box count, cubic meters, weight, and ready date.

Missing or inaccurate details are the most common reason a quote changes after booking.

Getting the HS code right matters for more than pricing. It determines the duty rate US Customs and Border Protection applies at entry, and errors are a frequent cause of clearance delays.
