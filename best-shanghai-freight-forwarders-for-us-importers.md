### Which Services Matter Most for Your Shipment Volume and Destination?

Start your shortlist with container volume. A business shipping one or two 40-foot containers a year has different needs from one running weekly LCL consolidations into three Amazon warehouses.

If your cargo won’t fill a container, LCL consolidation keeps costs down because you pay only for the space you use. Businesses with steady, larger volumes often get better per-unit economics with dedicated FCL service, particularly on the [Shanghai-to-USA lane](https://aeblogistics.com/freight-forwarder-shanghai-to-usa/), where container availability shifts with seasonal demand.

### Can the Forwarder Handle FBA Preparation and Delivery Appointments?

Confirm this directly. FBA prep isn’t a standard feature, even among established China forwarders. Check whether the service includes FBA labeling, UPS labeling where applicable, palletization, inspection support, and delivery-appointment scheduling through Amazon’s Carrier Appointment Request Portal.

For LCL shipments, forwarders usually book appointments after cargo reaches a warehouse in the destination country, not at origin. If you’re using your own carrier, ask whether the forwarder can still coordinate the appointment using your bill of lading, Amazon FBA booking form, and carrier manual.

### Who Coordinates Customs Clearance and Acts as Importer of Record?

You remain the Importer of Record and responsible for duties and taxes, no matter which forwarder you use. Amazon doesn’t act as Importer of Record on FBA shipments, and it won’t accept cargo that arrives with unpaid duties, taxes, or shipping charges.

A forwarder typically needs a Power of Attorney before it can file customs entries for you. Ask if the company arranges an annual customs bond or lets you use its own bond, and how it estimates duties and taxes based on your HS code and cargo value.

### What Shipment Milestones and Exception Updates Will You Receive?

Visibility matters most when something goes wrong, not when everything runs smoothly. Ask which milestones you’ll see (accepted, in transit, export customs, export departure, import entry, import arrival, delivered) and whether updates appear in a live tracking system or only arrive when you ask.

A dedicated account manager often responds faster than a general support queue during a customs hold or missed appointment, since they already know your shipment’s history.

### How Can You Vet Forwarder Reliability and Market Presence?

Third-party review platforms like Clutch provide valuable feedback on freight forwarders in Shanghai. Checking verified project details and client ratings on Clutch clarifies how well a provider communicates and resolves operational issues.

Evaluating past project details helps you assess a partner’s practical China freight forwarding performance before entrusting them with your inventory.

## How to Compare Shanghai-to-US Shipping Quotes

Start with the same shipment details for every quote. Two forwarders can quote the same cargo at very different prices if one assumes FOB terms and the other assumes EXW, or if one bundles customs brokerage while the other lists it separately.

### What Shipment Details Do You Need for Comparable Quotes?

Give every forwarder the same core details: origin and destination, Incoterms (FOB or EXW), HS code and cargo value, box count, cubic meters, kilograms, and your estimated cargo-ready date. Missing any of these, especially the HS code or Incoterms, often leads to quote discrepancies and surprise charges once the shipment is underway.

### What Does Each DDP or Door-to-Door Quote Actually Include?

DDP, or Delivered Duty Paid, should cover export declaration, international transport, import customs clearance, duty and tax payment, and final delivery. A door-to-door service without the DDP label may stop at import clearance, leaving you to handle duties, taxes, or last-mile delivery as separate charges.

Ask each forwarder to confirm in writing whether the quoted price includes duties and taxes or whether they’ll bill you separately after the shipment clears customs.

### Which Destination Charges and Delivery Accessorials Could Be Extra?

Common add-ons include liftgate service for locations without a loading dock, which AEB Logistics lists at an additional USD 100, as well as residential delivery surcharges and other accessorial fees. These charges usually aren’t part of a base freight quote, so ask whether your delivery address qualifies for any of them.

### Is Cargo Insurance Included in Your Shipping Quote?

Carrier liability policies under maritime law provide minimal reimbursement if your container is lost or damaged at sea. Inquiring about standalone cargo insurance protects the full commercial value of your inventory.

Many forwarders offer cargo insurance as an optional line item, typically costing a small fraction of the declared shipment value.

### Does the Quoted Transit Time Cover Port-to-Port or Door-to-Door Service?

Port-to-port transit times cover only the ocean or air leg. Door-to-door estimates also include trucking, customs processing, and final delivery at both ends.

Published general estimates put standard air freight from China to the US at roughly 8 to 10 days and ocean freight door to door at roughly 30 to 40 days. Actual times vary with customs processing, port congestion, and carrier schedules, so ask which definition applies to the quoted transit time before comparing it with another quote.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which Shanghai freight forwarder is a good fit for a small US importer?

A forwarder with a dedicated account manager and bundled customs coordination often works well for smaller importers without in-house logistics staff. AEB Logistics structures its service this way: one contact manages quotes, documentation, and delivery questions for each shipment.

### Does a freight forwarder need an office in Shanghai to handle my shipment?

A physical Shanghai office isn’t required, but local pickup coordination and warehouse access near your suppliers can reduce delays. Confirm whether the company handles factory pickup directly in the Shanghai region or hires a local partner, since service quality can vary with that arrangement.

### Which freight forwarders can deliver from Shanghai to Amazon FBA in the US?

Several forwarders on this list, including AEB Logistics, SINO Shipping, and DFH Logistics, offer FBA-specific services like labeling, palletization, and delivery-appointment scheduling. Confirm appointment details directly, since LCL shipments usually need to reach a destination warehouse before anyone can schedule an appointment.

### Does DDP shipping from Shanghai include US import duties and customs clearance?

DDP shipping is designed to cover duty and tax payment along with customs clearance, but you remain legally responsible for those costs as the importer, even when a forwarder pays on your behalf. Confirm in writing that your quote covers duties and taxes rather than listing them as a separate line item.

### How long does freight forwarding from Shanghai to the US take?

Standard air freight from China to the US generally takes about 8 to 10 days, while ocean freight door to door takes roughly 30 to 40 days. Customs processing, port congestion, carrier schedules, and seasonal demand all affect actual transit time, so treat published ranges as estimates rather than guarantees.

### Should I choose a freight forwarder or book directly with an ocean carrier?

A freight forwarder suits most SMB importers. It brings customs coordination, documentation, and delivery together under one contact, while booking directly with a carrier like Maersk means arranging customs brokerage and last-mile delivery separately. Carrier-direct booking can work for businesses with high, consistent container volumes and logistics staff who can coordinate the other pieces.
