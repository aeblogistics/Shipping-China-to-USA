## Combine Shanghai Supplier Orders into One Shipping Plan

 Combining orders from several Shanghai suppliers into one shipment can lower your per-unit freight cost and simplify customs paperwork. But it only works when supplier timelines and products are compatible.

 Streamlining international shipping requires aligning factory schedules, warehouse staging, and vessel departures. We build the plan around your actual cargo-ready dates, not the assumption that every order belongs in the same container.

 Multi-supplier consolidation, LCL, FCL, and air freight suit different combinations of volume, urgency, and supplier count. To choose the right option, start by checking how many suppliers you’re working with and when each can have goods ready.

### Multi-Supplier Consolidation for Alibaba Buyers and US Importers

 Buyers sourcing from more than one Shanghai factory, including through Alibaba and 1688, can have goods picked up from each supplier and brought into one coordinated international shipping plan. Before pickup begins, we confirm each supplier’s address, cargo-ready date, and carton details.

 The products don’t need to come from the same marketplace or vendor type. Even after the cartons are physically combined, the shipment still needs accurate commercial invoices, HS codes, and values for each supplier’s goods.

### LCL Shipping for Smaller Combined Orders

 LCL shipping works when your combined cargo doesn’t fill a full container. A less than container load (LCL) shipment lets you share container space with other shippers, so you pay for the volume your consolidated order uses rather than renting a full 20-foot or 40-foot container.

 This option suits buyers combining a handful of suppliers with moderate carton counts. Our [sea freight china usa](https://aeblogistics.com/sea-freight-china-usa/) service includes door-to-door freight, customs clearance coordination, and delivery to Amazon FBA.

### FCL Shipping for Dedicated Container Space

 FCL shipping makes sense when your consolidated supplier orders add up to enough volume to justify a dedicated container. You can choose a 20-foot, 40-foot, or 40-foot high-cube container. Because the container isn’t shared with other shippers, it usually means fewer handling touchpoints between Shanghai and your US destination.

 Larger combined orders, or cargo that needs extra security, tend to suit FCL better than LCL.

 For high-volume cargo, dedicated container space serves as an economical ocean freight solution that simplifies multi-supplier receiving.

### Air Freight for Time-Sensitive Inventory

 Air freight suits consolidated orders where speed matters more than cost per kilogram. An air shipment from Hong Kong to Los Angeles can take around five days, while general industry guidance puts standard air freight closer to 7 to 10 days, depending on the route and destination.

 If you urgently need one supplier’s goods, shipping that SKU by air while the rest travels by ocean freight is often more practical than delaying the entire order.

## How Much Does Consolidated Shipping from Shanghai Cost?

 Consolidated shipping from Shanghai usually costs less per unit than shipping each supplier’s goods separately. But to make a fair comparison, include every charge, not just the freight rate on the quote. A 20-foot container, a shared LCL pallet, and an air shipment all have different prices, and combining suppliers changes the math again.

### Compare Total Landed Cost, Not Just the Freight Rate

 Landed cost includes everything needed to get consolidated cargo from Shanghai suppliers to your US door: freight, customs brokerage, duties, insurance, and destination handling. In international shipping, a quote that shows only the base ocean freight or air rate leaves out the number that matters for budgeting.

 Fuel surcharges, cargo value (which affects both insurance and customs valuation), and destination charges all contribute to the total. Cargo insurance can give your combined shipment extra protection against transit risk, which matters when goods from several suppliers travel in the same container.

### Pickup, Storage, Handling, and Destination Charges

 Consolidation adds steps that separate shipments don’t need: domestic pickup from each Shanghai-area factory, warehouse receiving, short-term storage while you wait for the last supplier, and repacking or labeling if needed. Each step may appear as a separate line item.

 We can include customs brokerage in your quote. It usually runs around USD 125, depending on how many commodities are in the shipment. Locations without a loading dock may need liftgate service, billed at USD 100.

### When Consolidation Saves Money, and When Separate Shipping Makes Sense

 Consolidation tends to pay off when suppliers are reasonably close together, cargo-ready dates line up within a few days, and the products can travel together without regulatory conflicts. It makes less sense when one supplier is weeks behind the others. In that case, waiting can cost more in delayed inventory than shipping that order on its own.

 Separate shipments may also work better when the final US destinations differ, or when one SKU needs air freight while the rest can wait for ocean freight.

 Weighing these trade-offs is a routine part of international shipping when managing varied production schedules across China.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does consolidated shipping from Shanghai to the USA take?

 Transit time depends on the shipping mode and how many suppliers’ cargo-ready dates you’re waiting on. Published general estimates put ocean freight door to door at approximately 30 to 40 days and standard air freight at approximately 8 to 10 days. Port congestion, customs processing, and supplier delays can change the actual timeline, though.

### Can you combine orders from Shanghai suppliers and factories elsewhere in China?

 Yes, products don’t have to come from a single city to be consolidated into one shipment. Cargo can move from inland factories or other China ports into Shanghai for consolidation. The key requirement is that the shipment is legally and operationally combined under one export plan.

### What happens if one supplier misses the agreed cargo-ready date?

 A late supplier can delay the whole consolidated shipment if the plan waits for every order to arrive together. We track each supplier’s status against the shipment manifest. If a delay looks significant, shipping the ready goods separately is often more practical than holding everything.

### Is consolidated LCL shipping cheaper than separate supplier shipments?

 Consolidated LCL shipping often costs less per unit than shipping each supplier separately because you share container space and some origin and destination charges. It isn’t automatically cheaper in every case, particularly when suppliers are far apart or require separate handling.

### How are US tariffs and import duties calculated for consolidated goods?

 Each supplier’s goods still need accurate HS codes, product descriptions, and values, even after the cartons are physically combined, since duties are assessed per commodity. We provide HS/HTS code guidance and import duty estimation support once we confirm shipment information. Importers remain responsible for the duties and taxes owed, though.

### Can AEB Logistics deliver consolidated shipments directly to Amazon FBA?

 Yes, we handle complete Amazon FBA delivery for consolidated shipments from Shanghai directly to fulfillment centers. We offer FBA labeling and inspection support, and schedule delivery appointments through Amazon’s Carrier Appointment Request Portal. For LCL shipments, we schedule appointments after cargo arrives at our US warehouse, and we can split shipments for multiple centers.
