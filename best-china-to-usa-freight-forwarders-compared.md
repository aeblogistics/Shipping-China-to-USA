### FCL, LCL, and Multi-Supplier Consolidation Capabilities

Booking a full container load gives you exclusive container space, making FCL shipping ideal for high-volume cargo. LCL places goods in a shared container so you pay only for occupied volume.

Importers on Reddit point out that LCL allows even single pallets to move. Choosing between them depends on total cubic meters and inventory turnaround schedules.

Multi-supplier consolidation is one area where forwarders differ. AEB Logistics lists consolidated shipping for cargo from multiple suppliers, including multi-supplier consolidation within FCL. For other providers, confirm where consolidation happens, how long the warehouse will hold your cargo, and whether supplier deliveries have a cutoff date.

Quotes are only comparable when they cover identical services. Check origin pickup, export clearance, ocean freight, destination terminal charges, customs brokerage, and final door-to-door shipping.

## How to Choose a Forwarder for Your Shipment

Match the forwarder’s operating model to your shipment volume and how much coordination you want to handle yourself. Smaller importers often do better with a forwarder that offers a named contact and consolidation. Larger accounts may benefit from a global network with negotiated contracts.

### Should You Consolidate Supplier Orders or Book a Full Container?

Consolidate when each supplier’s cargo is too small to fill a container. As your cargo grows, FCL can offer better per-unit costs and more security. Ask for LCL and FCL quotes on the same cargo, then compare totals that include destination charges.

### How Do Shanghai and Other Chinese Ports Affect Your Routing?

Your supplier’s location usually determines the natural origin. Shanghai, Ningbo-Zhoushan, Shenzhen, and Qingdao serve different manufacturing regions. Trucking cargo to a distant port can cost more than shipping from the nearest one, so ask the forwarder to price both options.

### What Information Do You Need for Comparable Freight Quotes?

Give every freight forwarder from China to USA prospects identical shipment details. Provide your commercial invoice, packing list, HS codes, volume, weight, and cargo-ready dates.

Accurate packing details prevent unexpected billing discrepancies. State whether you require CIF, FOB, or DDP terms to ensure equivalent pricing across providers.

### Who Handles Importer Responsibilities, Duties, and Customs Entry?

The importer remains responsible for duties and taxes, even when working with a customs broker. For ocean shipments, timely Importer Security Filing avoids severe customs penalties.

In Amazon FBA logistics, Amazon will not act as the Importer of Record. They reject deliveries with unpaid duties, taxes, or freight handling charges.

### What Should You Confirm About Storage, Claims, and Document Release?

Ask about free storage days, port congestion surcharges, warehouse rates, and cargo insurance options. Clarify claim filing procedures and release schedules for the Bill of Lading.
