Shipping China to USA is a major route in global trade, connecting businesses of all sizes. If you're moving products between these two countries, knowing the basics can save you money and spare you headaches.

Shipping from China to the USA can take **1–2 days** with express couriers, **8–10 days** by air freight, or **30–40 days** by ocean freight. Costs range from **$3–5 per kilogram** for air, and flat rates for full containers by sea.

Your best shipping method depends on shipment size, budget, and how fast you need your goods. Transit times shift depending on ports, customs, and market conditions.

### Sea Freight Options (FCL & LCL)

Sea freight is the go-to for shipping China to USA if you have big or heavy loads.

#### Full Container Load (FCL)

| Container | Price Range | Transit Time |
|---|---|---|
| 20-foot | $3,640–$4,500 | 18–35 days |
| 40-foot | $5,200–$7,600 | 18–35 days |

> 💡 FCL makes sense if you fill at least **60–70%** of a container.

#### Less Than Container Load (LCL)

- Pay only for the space you use
- ~$382 per cubic meter to the West Coast
- Transit: **22–40 days** (longer due to consolidation handling)

## 🛃 Customs Clearance & Import Regulations

### Required Import Documents

| Document | Prepared By | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial Invoice | Supplier | Product details, quantities, values, country of origin |
| Packing List | Supplier | Carton numbers, weights, dimensions |
| Bill of Lading / AWB | Carrier | Proof of shipment and contract of carriage |
| Certificate of Origin | Supplier | Verifies where goods were manufactured |
| ISF 10+2 Filing | Freight Forwarder | Ocean only — must file **24h before vessel departs China** |
| Entry Summary (Form 7501) | Customs Broker | Filed within 15 days of US arrival |
| Power of Attorney | Importer | Authorizes broker to act on your behalf |

> ⚠️ **Missing ISF filing = up to $5,000 penalty per violation.**

---

### HS Codes, Tariffs & Duties

```
📌 HS Code / HTS Code  → Classifies your product and determines duty rate
📌 Standard Duty       → Based on product category
📌 Section 301 Tariff  → Additional China-specific tariffs (varies by product)
📌 Current Rate        → 30% on most Chinese goods (through August 2026)
📌 MPF                 → Merchandise Processing Fee (0.3464% of value, min $31.67)
📌 HMF                 → Harbor Maintenance Fee (ocean only, 0.125% of value)
```

#### Duty Calculation Example

```
Product Value:          $10,000
Standard Duty (5%):     $500
Section 301 (25%):      $2,500
MPF (0.3464%):          $34.64
HMF (0.125%):           $12.50
─────────────────────────────────
Total Import Cost:       $13,047.14
```

> ⚠️ Wrong HS code classification = incorrect duties + possible customs hold.
> Always verify your HTS code before shipping.

---

### Working with Customs Brokers

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Grant Authority** | Sign a Power of Attorney so broker can file under your EIN |
| **Provide Documents** | Commercial invoice, packing list, product details — before arrival |
| **Broker Files Entry** | Submits documents and pays duties on your behalf |
| **Goods Released** | CBP releases shipment after review and duty payment |
| **Broker Fee** | Typically $50–$200 per entry depending on complexity |
| **Customs Bond** | Required for shipments valued over $2,500 |

```
💡 Pro Tip: Send documents to your broker BEFORE the shipment arrives
           to avoid port storage fees and unnecessary delays.
```

## 🤝 Choosing a Freight Forwarder

### Role of Freight Forwarders

A freight forwarder manages the entire shipping process from Chinese manufacturer to US destination:

```
📦 Factory Pickup          → Coordinates collection from your supplier
📋 Documentation           → Prepares and files all required paperwork
🚢 Carrier Booking         → Negotiates space on vessels or aircraft
🛃 Customs Clearance       → Files ISF, entry documents, and duties
```

## 🛠️ Practical Tips & Common Challenges

### Handling Bulk Shipments

| Scenario | Recommended Option |
|---|---|
| Cargo fills 60–70%+ of container | FCL — pay flat rate, avoid per-unit overhead |
| Cargo fills less than a container | LCL — pay only for space used |
| Multiple suppliers, smaller volumes | Consolidation service via 3PL |

#### Packing Tips for Bulk Cargo

```
⚖️  Heavy goods     → Ensure even weight distribution across container floor
🔮  Fragile items   → Add corner protection, void fill, and clear labeling
📐  Calculate CBM   → Length (m) × Width (m) × Height (m) = Cubic Meters
📦  Dense packing   → Fewer, tighter cartons = lower volumetric weight charges
```

---

### Selecting Container Types

| Container Type | Interior Height | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard 20ft** | 8.5 ft | Dense, heavy cargo |
| **Standard 40ft** | 8.5 ft | Large volume, heavy goods |
| **High Cube 40ft** | 9.5 ft | Bulky, lightweight goods (furniture, textiles) |
| **Refrigerated (Reefer)** | Varies | Perishable goods, temperature-sensitive items |
| **Open Top** | No roof | Oversized machinery |
| **Flat Rack** | No walls | Cargo that won't fit in a box container |

> 💡 Choose **High Cube** for lightweight, bulky products like furniture,
> plastics, and textiles — the extra foot of height adds significant capacity.

### How is shipping cost calculated per kilogram?

Carriers use **whichever is greater** — actual weight or volumetric weight.

```
Volumetric Weight Formula (Air Freight):
L (cm) × W (cm) × H (cm) ÷ 6,000 = Volumetric Weight (kg)

Example:
50cm × 40cm × 30cm ÷ 6,000 = 10 kg volumetric weight

Additional Fees to Watch For:
• Fuel Surcharge
• Security Screening Fee
• Remote Delivery Surcharge
• Customs Clearance Fee
→ These can add $15–$25 per shipment on top of base rates
```
