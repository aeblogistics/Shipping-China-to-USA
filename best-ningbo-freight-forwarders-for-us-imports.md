### DDP Availability and US Customs Responsibilities

DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) shifts the burden of paying US duties and taxes to the forwarder. The importer receives one all-in invoice instead of a separate customs bill later. DTFU Logistics advertises DDP shipping to the USA, UK, and EU, with import taxes and duties handled as part of the service.

Importers remain ultimately responsible for duties owed to US Customs and Border Protection, even when a forwarder pays them upfront under a DDP arrangement. Confirm whether “DDP” in a quote actually includes duty payment or only covers transportation and brokerage fees. The term gets used loosely across the industry.

## How to Choose a Forwarder for Your US Import Shipment

### Should You Choose FCL, LCL, or Air Freight?

Match the shipping mode to your cargo volume and timeline. FCL suits shipments large enough to fill a 20-foot, 40-foot, or 40-foot high-cube container, and it offers a lower per-unit cost with less cargo handling. LCL works for smaller volumes, combining your freight with other shippers’ cargo in a shared container.

Air freight costs more per kilogram but moves faster, making it useful for time-sensitive or high-value goods. DTFU Logistics estimates air freight from Ningbo to the US West Coast at [3 to 6 days](https://www.dtfulogistics.com/freight-forwarder-in-ningbo/), compared with 15 to 20 days for FCL sea freight on the same route. A [sea freight option between China and the USA](https://aeblogistics.com/sea-freight-china-usa/) remains more economical for bulkier, less urgent cargo.

### What Information Makes Freight Quotes Comparable?

To compare quotes, provide the same details each time: origin and destination, Incoterm (FOB or EXW), HS code and cargo value, box count, cubic meters and kilograms, and estimated cargo-ready date. If any of these details are missing, comparing quotes becomes guesswork. Two forwarders quoting different assumed weights or volumes aren’t giving you comparable prices.

Ask each forwarder to quote the exact same shipment details, including whether cargo insurance is built in. A [DDP shipping quote from China to the USA](https://aeblogistics.com/ddp-shipping-quotes-china-to-usa/) should say whether duties and taxes are included or estimated separately. That single line item often explains a price gap between otherwise similar quotes.

Aligning these details gives you an accurate total landed cost to evaluate profitability across different freight proposals.

### Who Will Be the Importer of Record and Pay Duties?

The importer remains legally responsible for duties and taxes owed to US Customs, even under a DDP arrangement where a forwarder pays them upfront. For Amazon FBA shipments, Amazon doesn’t act as Importer of Record and will reject shipments that arrive with unpaid duties, taxes, or shipping charges.

Confirm in writing who signs as Importer of Record, who holds the customs bond, and whether the forwarder needs a Power of Attorney to clear goods on your behalf. These details determine who’s liable if customs flags a shipment for additional duty assessment after arrival.

### How Can You Verify a Forwarder’s Credentials and Service Commitments?

Check that the company has registered business status in China and any relevant Amazon partner designations, such as membership in the Amazon Service Provider Network. You can verify a legitimate China-based logistics company through China’s National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System at gsxt.gov.cn, which lists registration details for logistics entities operating legally in the country.

Ask for references on recent USA shipments and request a written breakdown of all quoted charges before signing a booking confirmation. A forwarder willing to itemize costs and explain each line is generally easier to work with than one offering only a bundled total.

### Which Destination Charges and Delivery Requirements Should You Confirm?

Confirm accessorial charges before booking. They can add meaningfully to a landed cost that looked competitive at the freight-quote stage. Liftgate service for locations without a loading dock can run an additional $100, while residential deliveries or pickups often carry separate accessorial fees.

Ask whether final-mile delivery uses local warehousing and distribution, LTL trucking, parcel couriers, or a dedicated Amazon FBA drop-off. Each option has different timing and costs. A forwarder offering [door-to-door delivery from Ningbo to the USA](https://aeblogistics.com/china-to-usa/ningbo-to-usa-door-to-door-shipping/) should specify which destination fees the quoted price includes.

### What should a DDP quote from Ningbo to the USA include?

### Can these freight forwarders deliver directly to Amazon FBA warehouses?
