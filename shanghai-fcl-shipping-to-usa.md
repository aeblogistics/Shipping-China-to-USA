## Choose the Right Container and Delivery Scope

The right container depends on your cubic meters (CBM), cargo weight, and how far you want AEB Logistics to carry the shipment. We book 20-foot, 40-foot, and 40-foot high-cube containers for full container load (FCL) shipments from Shanghai. Once we see your carton data, we’ll confirm the best fit.

### 20ft, 40ft, or 40ft High Cube: Which Fits Your Cargo?

A standard 20ft container holds about 33 CBM and suits dense, heavy cargo. For temperature-sensitive commodities like food products or perishables, a specialized reefer container can also be arranged. A 20-foot container remains the standard choice for heavy, compact manufactured goods.

A 40-foot container holds about 67 CBM, roughly double the space, which often lowers the cost per CBM. A 40-foot high cube holds about 76 CBM. Its extra height helps light, bulky goods fill the box before they reach weight limits.

    

| Container | Approx. capacity | Good fit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 20ft container | 33 CBM | Dense, heavy cargo (or reefer container for cold chain) |
| 40ft | 67 CBM | Larger orders, lower cost per CBM |
| 40ft High Cube | 76 CBM | Light, bulky goods |

### FCL vs. LCL: Which Offers Better Value for Your Shipment?

When evaluating FCL vs LCL, cargo volume and timing guide your decision. Reliable FCL shipping China to USA gives you a sealed container to yourself at a fixed rate, so every extra CBM lowers your unit cost.

Less than container load (LCL) puts your cargo in a shared container where you pay only for the volume you use. However, LCL shipping China to USA routes pass through a container freight station (CFS) at both ends, adding handling steps.

If your volume is close to the break-even point, ask our freight forwarder team to price both options. We consolidate LCL shipping consignments and can combine goods from several suppliers when volumes make sense.

### How Incoterms and Customs Responsibilities Affect Your Total

Your Incoterms 2020 term sets who pays for what. Under FOB Shanghai, your supplier delivers the container onto the vessel, and you pay for ocean freight, destination charges, duties, and delivery. Under EXW, you also cover inland trucking in China and export clearance. With DDP shipping, freight, duties, and delivery come together in one price.

You, as the importer, remain responsible for duties and taxes. For Amazon FBA deliveries, Amazon doesn’t act as importer of record and won’t accept shipments with unpaid duties, taxes, or shipping charges.

## Shanghai Departure Routes and US Delivery Timelines

Ocean transit from Shanghai to the US West Coast usually takes about 14 to 20 days port to port. East Coast routes take longer, often 28 to 40 days. Current ocean freight rates and vessel schedules heavily influence route selection.

While Shanghai Port is the world’s busiest hub, key China ports like Ningbo-Zhoushan, Shenzhen, Qingdao, and Tianjin also support trans-Pacific routes. We compare carrier allocations across these hubs to secure competitive transit times.

### Port-to-Port Transit vs. Your Complete Delivery Schedule

Port-to-port time covers only the vessel journey. Your delivery date also depends on factory pickup, export clearance, terminal release, drayage, and your receiving appointment. Plan backward from the date you need the goods in your warehouse, not from the vessel’s arrival date.
