### What Amazon FBA Preparation and Delivery Services Are Included?

FBA-specific work, such as labeling, inspection, and delivery-appointment scheduling, separates e-commerce specialists from general cargo movers. Confirm whether the forwarder labels goods in China or at a US warehouse before the final FBA leg.

Appointments booked through Amazon’s Carrier Appointment Request Portal need accurate shipment data in advance. For LCL shipments, the forwarder typically schedules delivery once the goods reach a destination-country warehouse. Check this before committing to a forwarder for recurring restocks.

### Who Handles Customs Entry, Duties, and Importer of Record Responsibilities?

You remain the importer of record even when a forwarder files your customs entry. You’re legally responsible for paying duties and taxes, regardless of which company prepares the paperwork.

Amazon doesn’t act as Importer of Record for FBA shipments and won’t accept inventory with unpaid duties, taxes, or shipping charges. A [freight forwarder offering full customs support](https://aeblogistics.com/freight-forwarders-china-to-usa-shipping-with-full-customs-support/) should explain bond options, HS code classification, and duty estimates before your cargo ships, not after it arrives.

### How Are Shipment Updates and Delivery Exceptions Managed?

Real tracking means status updates at each customs and transit milestone, not just a tracking number that goes quiet for weeks. Look for a forwarder that flags shipments at stages like Export Customs, Import Entry, and Import Arrival, so you can see where a delay is happening.

When something goes wrong, like a port inspection hold or a missed FBA delivery slot, one account manager who knows your shipment history can resolve it faster than a support queue.

### Which Capabilities Are Verified, and Which Require Confirmation?

Published service lists describe what a forwarder can generally do, but not necessarily what’s available for your specific lane or shipment size right now. Warehousing terms, free-storage periods, and DDP lane coverage change, and smaller or newer providers don’t always update their marketing pages right away.

Before booking, ask each forwarder directly which Shenzhen pickup addresses it serves, what the FBA appointment lead times are, and for an itemized quote. Confirming these details in writing gives you more protection than a general service description.

## How to Compare Quotes and Plan Your Shipment

For comparable quotes, give every forwarder the same shipment details. Even small differences in what you report can change the quote, so getting the details right upfront saves you from comparing numbers that aren’t really apples to apples.

### What Shipment Details Do You Need for Comparable Quotes?

Every forwarder needs the same core details: origin and destination, Incoterms (FOB or EXW), HS code, cargo value, box count, cubic meters, weight in kilograms, and estimated cargo-ready date. Leave anything out, and the quotes may differ once you actually book the shipment.

Accurate addresses, product descriptions, and HS codes matter because duty rates and customs processing depend directly on how you classify your goods. A [freight forwarder comparison](https://aeblogistics.com/freight-forwarder-comparison-for-china-to-usa-imports/) only works when every quote uses identical shipment data.

### When Should You Choose Air Freight, LCL, or FCL?

Air freight works well for urgent or high-value cargo when speed matters more than cost. Published estimates put standard air freight from China to the USA at roughly 8 to 10 days, while [ocean freight door to door runs around 30 to 40 days](https://aeblogistics.com/china-to-usa-freight-forwarders-top-10-services-for-e-commerce-brands/).

LCL suits businesses whose cargo won’t fill a full container. With [LCL ocean freight](https://aeblogistics.com/china-to-usa-freight-forwarding-port-to-port-ddp-and-fba/), you pay only for the space you use, while sharing the shipment with other shippers. FCL becomes more cost-effective once you have enough cargo to fill a 20-foot, 40-foot, or 40-foot high-cube container on its own.

### Which Charges and Exclusions Affect Total Landed Cost?

Your total landed cost includes freight, duties, taxes, insurance, and handling, not just the base freight rate. Ask whether the quote includes destination charges like drayage and container handling, or whether the forwarder bills those separately after arrival.

Liftgate service at locations without a loading dock can add a stated fee of around USD 100, and residential deliveries often come with extra accessorial charges. Customs brokerage is often quoted separately from freight and typically costs around USD 125, depending on how many commodities your shipment includes.

### How Much Buffer Should You Allow Before an Amazon Restock Deadline?

Build in extra time beyond the forwarder’s quoted transit estimate. Customs processing, port congestion, and carrier schedules can all push delivery past the original date, so treat published transit windows as a baseline, not a guarantee.

For ocean freight, plan your cargo-ready date weeks ahead of your Amazon restock deadline, not just days. [DDP shipping](https://aeblogistics.com/ddp-shipping-china-to-usa/) can simplify the customs side of the timeline, but it won’t change how long the physical transit takes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which Shenzhen freight forwarder is best for a small Amazon FBA seller?

A smaller FBA seller generally benefits most from a forwarder that offers one dedicated account manager, FBA labeling, and delivery-appointment coordination, rather than a large enterprise network built for bulk industrial freight. AEB Logistics fits that profile with its account-manager model and itemized quotes on request. Confirm current pricing and lane coverage directly before booking your first shipment.

### How much does shipping from Shenzhen to the USA cost?

Costs vary by shipping mode, cargo volume, and current market rates for shipping from China to the USA, so there isn’t one fixed price for every shipment. Ocean freight (FCL or LCL) tends to cost less per unit than air freight, while express courier options cost the most but move fastest. Request an itemized quote based on your box count, weight, and cubic meters to get an accurate number.

### How long does freight take from Shenzhen to a US Amazon fulfillment center?

Standard air freight from China to the USA typically takes about 8 to 10 days, while ocean freight door to door takes roughly 30 to 40 days. Customs processing, port congestion, and the carrier’s schedule can all affect the timing. Add buffer time before any Amazon restock deadline rather than planning around the minimum estimate.

### Does a DDP quote include all duties, tariffs, and Amazon delivery charges?

A DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) quote should cover freight, customs clearance, and duty payment, but confirm what’s included before booking. Practices vary by forwarder. Even with DDP, you remain the Importer of Record, so you’re ultimately responsible for paying duties and taxes correctly. Amazon won’t accept FBA shipments with unpaid duties, taxes, or shipping charges.

### Can these freight forwarders consolidate orders from multiple Chinese suppliers?

Many Shenzhen-area forwarders can combine cargo from several suppliers into one container or LCL shipment before shipping. This can lower per-unit freight costs and simplify paperwork compared with managing separate shipments from each factory. Check the forwarder’s current consolidation capacity and any related fees before combining multiple supplier orders in one shipment.

### Do I need a freight forwarder based in Shenzhen to ship from a Shenzhen supplier?

Your forwarder doesn’t need a Shenzhen headquarters if it can confirm pickup coverage at your supplier’s actual factory address. More important is whether it can reliably coordinate pickup, consolidation, and documentation from that location. Before assuming a forwarder has local coverage, ask about service in your supplier’s district.
