### 20-Foot, 40-Foot, and 40HQ Container Options

We book 20-foot, 40-foot, and 40-foot high-cube (40HQ) containers. A 20-foot box suits dense, heavy cargo, while a 40-foot box offers roughly double the space for a lower cost per cubic meter. A 40HQ adds height, which helps with light, bulky goods that fill the space before they reach weight limits.

Share your carton count, cubic meters, and kilograms, and we’ll recommend the container that fits.

### FCL vs. LCL: Which Fits Your Shipment?

Understanding FCL vs LCL helps determine the right balance of freight costs and transit times. FCL suits larger shipments, cargo needing security, and importers seeking lower per-unit shipping rates.

Less than container load (LCL) shipping places goods in a shared container where you pay only for used space. Evaluating FCL vs LCL ensures you avoid paying for empty container volume unnecessarily.

If your volume sits near the threshold between both modes, ask us to price both options. Comparing our [LCL shipping from China](https://aeblogistics.com/lcl-shipping-from-china/) option with an FCL quote shows where the break-even point falls for your cargo.

### What Changes Your Freight Rates and Total Landed Cost?

Several factors affect your freight rates and total landed cost:

- Origin port and U.S. destination port
- Container size and cargo weight
- Peak season demand and carrier schedules
- Duties and taxes based on your HS code and cargo value
- Cargo insurance and receiving requirements, such as a liftgate (USD 100 where no dock is available)
- Residential delivery or other accessorial fees
 

Landed cost is the full price of getting goods to your door, including duties. Accurate addresses, weights, dimensions, product descriptions, and HS codes help keep your quote close to your final invoice.

### DDP vs. Other Terms: Who Pays Duties and Destination Charges?

Under Incoterms such as FOB or EXW, the importer arranges and pays for transport and clearance from a set point. With DDP shipping, duty payment and destination charges are combined into one upfront price.

Opting for DDP shipping simplifies logistics, but importers must ensure customs entries remain compliant. We use accurate HS codes and honest cargo values on every entry.

Amazon FBA shipping requires that every delivery arrives with all duties, taxes, and destination fees fully paid. For U.S. imports, we can arrange an annual continuous bond or offer single-entry coverage case by case.

### Can I send urgent goods by air while the rest travel by ocean?

Yes. We offer air freight and air cargo services for time-sensitive or high-value goods alongside sea freight and ocean options.

For air shipments, freight costs are calculated based on chargeable weight, which compares actual gross weight against volumetric weight. Volumetric weight ensures light, bulky cargo is billed accurately according to the space it occupies on an aircraft.
