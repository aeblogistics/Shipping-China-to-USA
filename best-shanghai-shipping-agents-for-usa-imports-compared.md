     ### What Does a DDP Quote Actually Include?

A DDP quote should name four cost buckets: ocean or air freight, export customs in China, import customs and duties in the USA, and last-mile delivery. Some agents bundle all four into one number. Others, like Flexport, list freight, customs, and inland transport as separate line items for you to add up.

Before comparing numbers, ask every agent the same question: does the quote include the US customs bond, duty payment, and delivery appointment fees, or will they bill those separately later? ## How to Compare Quotes for a Shanghai-to-USA Shipment

Side-by-side quote comparisons only work when every agent prices the same shipment on the same terms. For reliable China to USA shipping, verify freight rates against a finalized commercial invoice. Mismatched Incoterms or missing destination charges are common reasons two quotes differ by thousands of dollars.

### Which Shipment Details Should You Send Each Agent?

Send every agent the same shipment packet, including a complete packing list and commercial invoice. Be sure to provide origin and destination, Incoterms (FOB or EXW), HS code, cargo value, dimensions, and cargo-ready date. Missing HS codes or inaccurate packing list details often cause the biggest quote discrepancies once cargo moves.

AEB Logistics requests this same information to prepare an accurate quote, and it’s a reasonable baseline for any [shipping agent from China to USA](https://aeblogistics.com/shipping-agent-from-china-to-usa/) you’re vetting. If your cargo includes multiple commodities, list each HS code separately rather than grouping them. Customs brokerage fees often depend on the number of commodities.

### Which Destination Charges and Delivery Fees Should You Confirm?

Confirm destination charges such as container drayage, liftgate service for locations without a loading dock, and residential delivery surcharges. Liftgate service can cost an extra USD 100 when needed, and residential deliveries and pickups often come with accessorial fees that basic ocean freight quotes leave out.

Ask whether the quote covers final-mile delivery to an Amazon FBA facility or only delivery to a US port or warehouse. The difference between “delivered to port” and “delivered to FBA dock” is where unexpected trucking costs often show up, especially for a shipment moving through [LCL](https://aeblogistics.com/lcl/) consolidation.

### Who Is Responsible for Duties, Taxes, and the Import Entry?

The importer of record remains legally responsible for duties and taxes, even when an agent handles the paperwork. Amazon doesn’t act as Importer of Record for FBA shipments and won’t accept cargo with unpaid duties, taxes, or shipping charges. Confirm who’ll file the entry and when you need to pay duties.

An agent needs a Power of Attorney to file customs entries on your behalf. Some providers offer an annual bond, while others let you use their own bond for US imports on a case-by-case basis. Clarify the details before cargo sails, not when it’s sitting at the port waiting for an entry filing. For FCL shipments, review how [FCL](https://aeblogistics.com/fcl/) customs coordination works so container drayage and entry timing line up.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which Shanghai shipping agent is best for an Amazon FBA shipment to the USA?

AEB Logistics works well for FBA shippers because it handles labeling, product inspection support, palletization, and appointment scheduling through Amazon’s Carrier Appointment Request Portal. For LCL shipments, it can schedule appointments once cargo reaches a warehouse in the destination country. That helps sellers who split inventory across multiple fulfillment centers.

### How do I compare DDP quotes from China-to-USA shipping agents?

Request the same shipment details from every agent: origin, destination, Incoterms, HS code, cargo value, box count, volume, weight, and cargo-ready date. Then check whether each quote bundles customs brokerage, duty payment, and last-mile delivery into one number or lists them separately for you to add up.

### Should I choose FCL or LCL for cargo leaving Shanghai?

FCL makes sense when your cargo fills or nearly fills a 20-foot or 40-foot container. You pay for the whole container but avoid consolidation handling. LCL works better for smaller volumes, since you pay only for the space your cargo uses in a shared container.

### How long does shipping from Shanghai to the USA take?

Ocean freight for shipping from China to USA destinations generally takes 30 to 40 days for door-to-door delivery. Standard air freight takes about 8 to 10 days, while express courier and express shipping options can arrive in 3 to 5 days.

### Can a shipping agent coordinate U.S. customs clearance and final delivery?

Most established agents can coordinate customs brokerage, but they’ll need a Power of Attorney on file before they file entries for you. Check whether final delivery goes to a warehouse, a residential address, or an Amazon FBA dock. Each destination has its own fees and scheduling requirements.
