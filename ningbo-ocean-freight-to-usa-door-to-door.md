### How FCL, LCL, and Delivery Location Affect Freight Rates

 Container type directly affects your freight rate. In modern container shipping, a dedicated 20-foot or 40-foot container (FCL) costs more upfront than shared LCL space, but your per-unit costs often drop as volume grows.

 Delivery location matters too. A West Coast address usually means a shorter ocean leg, while an East Coast or inland delivery adds trucking or rail time after the vessel discharges.

### FOB, EXW, and DDP: Who Pays Which Charges?

 Standard freight Incoterms determine who pays for what before the goods reach the port. Under FOB, your supplier covers costs up to loading on the vessel, and you take over from there. Under EXW, you’re responsible for pickup from the factory door, which means more coordination on your end.

 With DDP shipping, AEB Logistics handles duty payment upfront alongside duties and taxes, so you receive one all-in price instead of a customs bill waiting at delivery. Import duties and taxes remain the importer’s legal responsibility regardless of which Incoterm you choose; DDP just changes who pays and when.

## Choose the Right Container and Delivery Service

 The right container depends on your cargo volume, not a fixed rule of thumb. Full container shipments suit larger volumes and more sensitive cargo, while shared space works for smaller loads that don’t justify a dedicated box.

### FCL Shipping With 20-Foot, 40-Foot, and High-Cube Containers

 Full container load (FCL) gives you a sealed, dedicated container that no other shipper’s cargo touches. We arrange 20-foot, 40-foot, and 40-foot high-cube containers to suit your cargo’s weight and dimensions. FCL shipping often makes sense once your volume approaches half a 20-foot container, since you pay for the full box regardless of how much space you use.

 Fewer handling points also mean less risk of damage than with consolidated freight.

### LCL Shipping and Multi-Supplier Consolidation

 Less than container load (LCL) works for smaller shipments that don’t fill a container on their own. Your cargo shares space with other shippers’ general cargo, and you pay for the cubic meters you use.

 If you’re buying from more than one factory, multi-supplier consolidation combines cargo from different Zhejiang suppliers into one shipment. Our origin warehousing and consolidation services lower freight costs compared with shipping each order separately.

 Coordinated cargo consolidation does add handling time at origin and destination, so factor that into any fixed delivery window.

### Door Delivery to US Warehouses and Business Addresses

 Door delivery means your container or consolidated freight moves beyond the port and straight to your warehouse or business address, with inland trucking arranged on your behalf. We coordinate drayage and final-mile delivery, so you don’t have to arrange a separate trucking company after the vessel discharges. For locations without a loading dock, liftgate service is available for an additional USD 100.

### Air Freight and Express Courier Alternatives

 While ocean freight offers the lowest shipping rates, critical deadlines may call for air freight from Ningbo via Ningbo Lishe International Airport. For smaller product samples or urgent test inventory, express services through FedEx, DHL, or UPS provide rapid door delivery.

 West Coast ports like the Port of Los Angeles and the Port of Oakland have frequent, direct sailings from Ningbo, which typically means a shorter ocean transit than East Coast routings.
