In the realm of international logistics, shipping from China to the USA with complex cargo presents a different challenge than a standard carton shipment. Oversized machinery needs flat-rack booking and rigging coordination. Dangerous goods need UN classification, packing group documentation, and a broker who won’t flinch at an IMDG or 49 CFR filing.

Regulated goods, such as electronics with FCC requirements or products needing FDA prior notice, need customs support that goes beyond a basic entry.

**For most U.S. importers handling oversized, hazardous, or regulated cargo from China, a China-focused forwarder with in-house customs brokerage and Amazon FBA experience, such as AEB Logistics, offers the coordination depth that large global carriers often reserve for enterprise accounts.** Global forwarders like Kuehne+Nagel, DHL Global Forwarding, and Expeditors bring the scale and compliance infrastructure that regulated industries and high-volume programs need.

State-owned logistics giants like Sinotrans handle enormous container flows at origin ports. Meanwhile, mid-tier and specialist forwarders fill critical gaps for FBA sellers, project cargo, and businesses that want one account manager instead of a call center.

Picking the wrong partner on a complex shipment usually shows up as a stuck container, a rejected FBA delivery, or a demurrage bill nobody budgeted for. This comparison looks at ten forwarders through the lens of hazardous-cargo handling, oversize and project freight, customs depth, warehousing, and Amazon fulfillment support.

That way, you can match the shipment type to the right operator.

     ### Can the forwarder accept your cargo classification, dimensions, and handling requirements?

Start by confirming that the forwarder books the equipment your cargo needs. Oversized machinery may require flat-rack or open-top containers, while dangerous goods need a carrier that accepts the specific UN class and packing group listed on your safety data sheet.

Ask for written confirmation before booking, not just a verbal yes. If a forwarder hesitates over a DG classification question, look elsewhere for that shipment.

### How deep is its customs clearance and compliance support?

Look for in-house customs brokerage rather than a referral to a third party. Brokers who handle HTS classification, import-entry documents, and duty estimates directly can catch issues before goods reach the port, rather than after a hold notice arrives.

Brokers must also account for Section 301 tariffs that can drastically increase import duties on Chinese goods. While small e-commerce shipments might leverage the de minimis exemption under Section 321, commercial freight requires strict tariff compliance.

For regulated products, ask whether the forwarder has direct experience with FDA prior notice, FCC certification, or other agency requirements tied to your HS code. General customs experience doesn’t always cover agency-specific filings.

### Does its warehousing network match your inspection, storage, and distribution needs?

Prompt container drayage from congested marine terminals into local staging facilities prevents costly port storage charges. Warehousing near major U.S. ports supports consolidation, quality inspection, and staged distribution before final delivery.

That matters most for importers combining cargo from multiple suppliers or needing a pre-delivery inspection before goods reach a retail or FBA destination.

Confirm whether the forwarder uses its own facilities or third-party warehouses. That choice affects storage-rate stability and how quickly inventory can move once it lands.

### Can it support Amazon FBA fulfillment and appointment-controlled final delivery?

FBA delivery requires accurate labeling, correct palletization, and a booked appointment through Amazon’s Carrier Appointment Request Portal. Amazon doesn’t act as Importer of Record and won’t accept shipments with unpaid duties, taxes, or shipping charges, so the forwarder handling customs needs to sequence everything correctly.

Ask whether the forwarder can split inventory across multiple fulfillment centers and reschedule appointments if the shipment date shifts.

### What should an all-in China-to-USA freight quote include?

A usable quote lists origin pickup, export documents, ocean or air freight, destination charges, customs brokerage, and final delivery as separate line items. Buyers should determine their total landed cost under the agreed Incoterm, whether FOB, EXW, or DDP, because it determines who pays for each stage.

Provide a detailed packing list, the HS code, cargo value, box count, cubic meters, weight, and cargo-ready date when requesting quotes. Incomplete information is the most common reason quotes change after a shipment gets booked.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which freight forwarder is best for complex China-to-USA shipments?

The right choice depends on the type of complexity. AEB Logistics fits importers that need coordinated customs, warehousing, and Amazon FBA delivery with one account manager, while large industrial or hazardous shipments may suit forwarders like DSV or CEVA Logistics, which have dedicated project-cargo divisions.

### How do I find a freight forwarder in China for oversized or hazardous cargo?

Ask directly whether the forwarder has booked flat-rack or open-top containers and handled UN-classified dangerous-goods documents before. Request references or examples related to your specific cargo type rather than general shipping experience. Oversize and DG handling require different equipment and paperwork from standard container freight.

### Can a freight forwarder handle customs clearance for regulated goods entering the United States?

Yes, forwarders with in-house customs brokerage can manage import-entry documents, HTS classification, and duty estimates for regulated goods. Confirm that the broker has specific experience with your product’s agency requirements, such as FDA or FCC rules, because general customs experience doesn’t always cover those filings.

### Can freight forwarders deliver hazardous or oversized shipments to Amazon FBA warehouses?

Amazon FBA has strict labeling, packaging, and appointment requirements, and hazardous materials face additional restrictions under Amazon’s dangerous goods program. Work with a forwarder experienced in FBA delivery appointment scheduling, and confirm that your specific product category is eligible before booking freight.

### Is it cheaper to use a freight forwarder for international shipping from China?

A forwarder’s negotiated carrier rates and consolidated LCL space often reduce per-unit shipping costs compared with booking freight directly as a small shipper. The bigger savings often come from avoiding customs delays, storage fees, and rebooking costs when documentation or classification issues go unmanaged.

### What information do I need to request a China-to-USA freight quote?

Provide the origin and destination, Incoterms such as FOB or EXW, HS code, cargo value, box count, cubic meters, weight, and estimated cargo-ready date. Getting these details right helps prevent quote discrepancies and unexpected charges after you book the shipment.
