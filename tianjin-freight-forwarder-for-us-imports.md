### What Affects Air and Ocean Freight Rates?

Freight rates change based on cargo weight, volume, and the shipping lane you choose. Carriers price base ocean freight by container size for FCL or by cubic meter for LCL, then add fuel surcharges, port handling charges, and destination fees.

Air freight is priced by chargeable weight, using whichever is higher: actual weight or volumetric weight. Bulky, lightweight cargo often costs more to fly than dense cargo with the same actual weight.

Seasonal demand matters, too. Rates climb ahead of Chinese New Year and the pre-holiday retail rush, when carriers see a surge in bookings from northern China ports.

### Who Pays Duties, Taxes, and Cargo Insurance?

Importers often choose DDP shipping to simplify transit costs into a single invoice. When goods move under DDP shipping or other Incoterms, AEB Logistics estimates duties and taxes from your HS codes, though the importer of record remains responsible for compliance.

For Amazon FBA shipments, Amazon doesn’t act as Importer of Record. Amazon will reject shipments with unpaid duties, taxes, or freight charges, so settle those costs before goods reach a fulfillment center.

## Which Freight Service Fits Your Tianjin Shipment?

Selecting the right freight services in Tianjin depends on how much cargo you’re moving and how soon you need it. Choosing between ocean and air, or FCL and LCL, comes down to volume, timeline, and budget.

### FCL, LCL, and Multi-Supplier Consolidation

Full Container Load makes sense when your cargo fills enough space to justify a dedicated 20-foot, 40-foot, or 40-foot high-cube container. FCL keeps goods sealed in one box from Tianjin to the destination port, reducing handling and the risk of damage for larger orders.

Less than Container Load works for smaller shipments that don’t need a full box. AEB Logistics consolidates LCL cargo into shared containers, so you pay for the space your goods actually use.

For importers sourcing from multiple factories around Tianjin, cargo consolidation brings shipments from different suppliers together in one container. This can lower per-unit shipping costs and simplify customs paperwork compared to shipping each supplier’s goods separately.

### Air Freight for Time-Sensitive Imports

Air freight from Tianjin moves faster than ocean freight, but it costs more per kilogram. AEB Logistics’ general Amazon FBA guidance estimates air freight lead times of around 7 to 10 days, depending on the shipment and destination. That can work well for urgent restocks or higher-value goods where speed justifies the extra cost.

Ocean freight remains the economical choice for bulkier, less time-sensitive cargo. Regular sea freight departures help keep container shipping costs predictable across major trade lanes.
