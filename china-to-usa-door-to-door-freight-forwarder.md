## How Much Does Door-to-Door Shipping Cost?

**Door-to-door shipping costs from China to the USA depend on your cargo’s chargeable weight, shipping method, and how much destination handling your quote includes.** There’s no flat per-shipment rate. A 20-foot container, a shared LCL pallet, and an air shipment all use different pricing scales, so the number that matters is your total landed cost, not just the freight line.

### What Should a China to USA Freight Quote Include?

A complete quote should show what happens at every stage, not just the ocean or air freight charge.

Ask your forwarder to itemize:

- Factory pickup and export handling in China
- International freight (ocean or air)
- U.S. customs clearance coordination
- Destination handling and inland delivery
- Any accessorial fees, like liftgate service
 
We can include customs brokerage in quotes on request. It usually runs around USD 125, depending on how many commodities are in the shipment. Get the breakdown in writing before you approve anything, since each forwarder’s scope varies.

### How Do Air, LCL, and FCL Costs Compare?

Air freight costs more per kilogram but moves faster. Ocean freight (LCL or FCL) costs less per unit, though it takes longer to arrive.

Freight rates use chargeable weight, which means carriers bill whichever is greater: actual weight or volumetric weight. Ocean freight pricing is based on cubic meters (CBM). LCL suits smaller loads that don’t fill a container, while FCL works better for larger volumes where a dedicated 20-foot, 40-foot, or 40-foot high-cube container makes more sense per unit.

### What Changes the Total Landed Cost?

Fuel surcharges, destination charges, and terminal handling charges all add to the base freight rate. Cargo value also affects insurance and customs valuation. If you add cargo insurance for transit protection, higher-value shipments will carry higher insurance costs.

## Choose the Right Service for Your Shipment

**The right shipping method depends on how urgent your cargo is and how much volume you’re moving, not on which option has the lowest sticker price.** When planning shipping from China to USA ports and inland hubs, express courier gets small, urgent parcels there fastest. Ocean freight costs less for bulk cargo that can wait a few weeks.

Most importers land somewhere between the two, depending on the shipment.

### Ocean Freight: LCL, FCL, and Cargo Consolidation

LCL (Less than Container Load) sea freight lets you ship without renting a full container. We combine your cargo with other shippers’ freight in a shared container, so you pay only for the space you use. It works well for smaller volumes and includes door-to-door transportation, customs clearance coordination, and delivery to Amazon FBA.

FCL (Full Container Load) suits larger shipments or cargo that needs extra security. You can choose a 20-foot, 40-foot, or 40-foot high-cube container. A dedicated container stays sealed from pickup through delivery, which usually means fewer handling touchpoints and a lower per-unit cost at higher volumes.

If you’re sourcing from more than one factory, cargo consolidation can combine shipments from multiple suppliers into one container where it makes sense. That can cut per-unit freight costs compared with shipping each supplier’s cargo separately.

### Air Freight and Express Shipping for Urgent Cargo

Air freight suits commercial shipments where speed matters more than cost per kilogram. An air shipment from Hong Kong to Los Angeles can take around five days, while standard air freight across routes generally takes closer to 8 to 10 days. Ocean freight on the same route can take at least three weeks, so air freight makes sense for product launches or urgent replenishment.

Express courier services like UPS, DHL, and FedEx work best for samples or small, urgent shipments, which generally arrive in about 3 to 5 days. These carriers bundle pickup, customs, and delivery into one service. But they charge the most per kilogram, so express works better for samples than full inventory restocks.

### DDP Options and Delivery to Amazon FBA

Under DDP shipping, duties and taxes get settled before your cargo arrives, so you won’t find a separate customs bill waiting at delivery. For sellers arranging DDP shipping from China to USA warehouses, choosing air freight DDP ensures rapid replenishment, while sea freight DDP keeps transport costs low.

Confirm the scope in writing before booking, since DDP coverage can vary from shipment to shipment.

For LCL shipments, we schedule FBA appointments after the cargo arrives at our destination-country warehouse. Amazon doesn’t act as Importer of Record, so unpaid duties or shipping charges will get a shipment rejected at the fulfillment center door.

## How Does Pickup-to-Delivery Coordination Work?

**We coordinate China-to-USA shipments in three connected stages: supplier pickup and export prep, international transport with customs entry, and warehousing through final delivery.** Missing paperwork at any stage can hold up the whole shipment, so we check the details before cargo leaves the factory.

### Supplier Pickup in Ningbo or Shanghai and Export Preparation

Pickup starts at your supplier’s factory or warehouse. We work with suppliers across major China origins, including Ningbo and Shanghai, and confirm carton counts and packaging condition before freight moves to the port or airport.

Before goods leave the supplier’s warehouse, you need a commercial invoice, a packing list, and accurate carton dimensions. The HS code (Harmonized System code) on the invoice determines the duty rate U.S. customs applies later. Getting it right at this stage can help you avoid a costly reclassification down the line.

### Documents, U.S. Customs Entry, and Importer Responsibilities

Once cargo leaves China, your freight forwarder issues a bill of lading for ocean shipments or an air waybill for air cargo. These documents travel with the shipment and support the import entry filed with U.S. Customs and Border Protection.

We work closely with a licensed US customs broker to coordinate customs brokerage and support documentation, including HS/HTS code guidance and duty estimates. We need a Power of Attorney if you ask us to handle customs clearance on your behalf.

Importers remain the Importer of Record and stay legally responsible for duties and taxes, regardless of who files the entry. We coordinate customs clearance, but we don’t operate as your customs broker of record, so confirm this scope with us directly for your shipment.

### Warehousing, Shipment Tracking, and Final Delivery

After customs releases the cargo, it moves to warehousing or straight to final delivery. Our integrated [warehousing](https://aeblogistics.com/warehousing/) and distribution services include storage space, pick-and-pack, labeling, palletization, and shipment splitting for deliveries to multiple Amazon warehouses.

Last-mile delivery uses LTL or small parcel services, coordinated through carrier partners like Schneider, J.B. Hunt, YRC, Landstar, North American, UPS Freight, and FedEx Freight. We coordinate with these carriers rather than running our own trucks. If your location doesn’t have a loading dock, you may need liftgate service, for an additional stated fee of USD 100.

You can [track your shipment](https://aeblogistics.com/can-i-track-my-shipment/) through milestones including Accepted, In Transit, Export Customs, Export Departure, Import Entry, Import Arrival, and Delivered.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does door-to-door shipping from China to the USA cost per kilogram?

Cost per kilogram depends heavily on the shipping method. Air freight and express courier charge by chargeable weight, while ocean freight charges by cubic meter (CBM). Request an itemized quote using your cargo’s actual weight, dimensions, and destination so the per-kilogram or per-CBM calculation reflects your shipment, not a generic average.

### How long does delivery from China to a U.S. warehouse take?

Transit time depends on the method. Our published general estimates are about 3 to 5 days for express courier, 8 to 10 days for standard air freight, and 30 to 40 days for ocean freight door to door. Customs processing, port congestion, weather, and seasonal demand can extend these windows.

### What is the fastest way to ship from China to the USA?

Express courier is the fastest option, typically arriving in about 3 to 5 days. It works well for samples or small, urgent parcels. For larger commercial shipments that need speed, air freight is the next fastest option, generally arriving in 8 to 10 days.

### Does door-to-door service include customs duties and taxes?

Door-to-door service doesn’t automatically include duties and taxes unless the quote specifically uses DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) terms. Importers remain legally responsible for duties and taxes regardless of the Incoterm used. For Amazon FBA shipments, Amazon won’t accept cargo that arrives with unpaid duties or shipping charges.

### Can I track my shipment from supplier pickup through delivery?

Yes, you can track every shipment in real time through milestones such as Accepted, In Transit, Export Customs, Export Departure, Import Entry, Import Arrival, and Delivered. Your dedicated account manager also provides proactive updates alongside the tracking system.
