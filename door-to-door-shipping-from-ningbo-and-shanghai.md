### How Do FCL, LCL, Air, and Express Charges Compare?

Full container load pricing is set per container. Less than container load freight is charged by cubic meter, so you pay only for the space your cargo uses in a shared container. Air and express pricing use chargeable weight, which is whichever is greater: actual weight or volumetric weight.

## Which Route and Service Fit Your Delivery Deadline?

The right service for a Ningbo or Shanghai shipment depends on cargo volume, urgency, and the U.S. port or inland hub it needs to reach. Ocean freight handles most planned restocks, while air and express step in when timing gets tight.

### FCL Shipping for Dedicated Containers and Larger Orders

FCL shipping gives your cargo a dedicated 20-foot, 40-foot, or 40-foot high-cube container that stays sealed from pickup through delivery. It works well for larger shipments, cargo that needs extra security, or importers looking to lower per-unit shipping costs.

With fewer handling touchpoints, FCL generally carries a lower damage risk than shared-container freight.

### LCL Shipping and Cargo Consolidation for Smaller Orders

LCL shipping suits businesses that don’t have enough cargo to fill a container. Cargo consolidation combines smaller shipments, sometimes from multiple suppliers around Ningbo and Shanghai, in one shared container. You pay for only the cubic meters you use.

Air freight and express courier move faster than sea freight, but they cost more per kilogram. ## How Are U.S. Customs, Documents, and Trade Terms Handled?

Navigating US customs clearance happens in two stages: export clearance in China before departure, then import customs clearance at the U.S. port before delivery. Missing paperwork at either stage can hold up cargo, so get the documents right before pickup rather than fixing them after the fact.

### Documents and Product Codes to Confirm Before Pickup

Every shipment needs a commercial invoice, a packing list, and a bill of lading for ocean freight or an air waybill for air cargo. Depending on the product, you may also need a certificate of origin.

The HS code, sometimes called the HTS code once it reaches U.S. Customs and Border Protection, determines your duty rate. Get it right before cargo leaves the factory to avoid a reclassification headache later.

### Importer Responsibilities, Customs Bonds, and Duty Payment

A customs broker files the entry with U.S. Customs and Border Protection, and most formal entries require a customs bond. Importers can use a single-entry bond or, if they import frequently, an annual customs bond. We can arrange an annual bond for U.S. imports or offer use of an AEB bond on a case-by-case basis.

The importer of record remains responsible for duties and taxes, no matter who files the paperwork. For Amazon FBA shipments, Amazon doesn’t act as the importer of record and will reject cargo with unpaid duties, taxes, or shipping charges. If you ask AEB Logistics to handle customs clearance on your behalf, you’ll need to provide a Power of Attorney.

### What Changes Under EXW, FOB, or DDP Terms?

Freight Incoterms determine who handles each step before cargo reaches the port. Under EXW, the buyer arranges pickup from the supplier’s door. Under FOB, the supplier gets cargo to the port, and the buyer takes over from there.

Under DDP (Delivered Duty Paid), the buyer settles duties and taxes before the shipment arrives, avoiding a separate customs bill at delivery. Selecting DDP shipping gives importers an all-inclusive rate with destination clearances settled upfront.

Confirm your Incoterm with us in writing before booking. It changes which charges appear in the freight quote and which go on a separate invoice.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the cheapest way to ship door to door from Ningbo or Shanghai to the USA?

Ocean freight usually has the lowest per-unit cost for shipments from Ningbo or Shanghai to the USA. Choose LCL for smaller volumes or FCL once your cargo fills roughly half a container.

Air freight and express courier cost more per kilogram but save weeks of transit time when a deadline rules out ocean shipping.

### How long does door-to-door shipping from Shanghai to the US take?

Ocean freight from Shanghai to the USA generally takes about 30 to 40 days door to door. Standard air freight takes closer to 8 to 10 days, while express courier takes about 3 to 5 days.

Customs processing, port congestion, and seasonal demand can affect transit times, so use these figures as planning estimates rather than fixed dates.

### Can AEB Logistics collect goods from multiple suppliers in Ningbo and Shanghai?

### Does door-to-door shipping include U.S. import duties?

Door-to-door service covers pickup, freight, customs coordination, and final delivery, but the Incoterm determines who pays import duties. Under DDP, duties are settled before arrival.

Under other terms, such as DAP, the importer pays duties separately. The importer of record remains responsible for them regardless of which term applies.
