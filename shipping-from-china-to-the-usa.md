## Shipping From China To The USA: Your Options Beyond Air and Ocean

 ![A cargo port with ships, an airplane, freight trains, and trucks transporting containers, illustrating various shipping methods from China to the USA.](./media/inline/v2-13v1jr-o0jxp.jpg)

When you’re planning your **[shipping from China to the USA](https://aeblogistics.com/shipping-freight-from-china-to-the-usa/)** strategy, you’ve got more choices than just air or ocean. There are actually six main ways to get your goods across the Pacific, and each fits different needs and budgets.

Express courier services (think DHL, FedEx, UPS) deliver in 3-5 days, door-to-door, with full tracking. These are great for urgent shipments under 200 kilograms or for high-value products that need extra care.

Standard air freight takes a bit longer—about 8-10 days—but costs $1-2 less per kilogram than express couriers. It’s a solid middle ground for time-sensitive goods that don’t need to be there overnight.

Ocean freight is the go-to for anything over 200 kilograms. You can book a full container (**[FCL shipping](https://aeblogistics.com/sea-freight-from-china-to-usa/)**) or just a part of one (LCL shipping). Transit usually takes 30-40 days, but port congestion can slow things down during busy seasons.

Rail freight sits between air and ocean in both speed and price. It’s especially handy for electronics and consumer goods heading to inland U.S. cities.

Expedited ocean services speed things up compared to regular ocean shipping, but still cost less than air. For the last stretch, truck transportation handles delivery to your door or warehouse.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Shipping From China To The USA

### What Are Current Freight Costs from China to America?

Shipping prices depend on your chosen method and cargo size. **Sea freight** usually costs $800 to $2,500 per container for standard shipments.

**Air freight** is pricier, running $3 to $8 per kilogram for regular cargo. Express services like DHL cost more—typically $6 to $12 per kilogram—but they’re much faster.

[Shipping rates change with fuel prices and seasonal demand](https://chinatopfreight.com/china-to-usa-shipping-guide-cost-transit/).

### How Long Do Shipments Take from China to America?

Your shipping method makes a big difference:

- **Ocean freight**: 25–40 days port to port
- **Air cargo**: Usually 5–10 days
- **Express courier**: 3–7 days for most big cities
 
Door-to-door service adds a little time for customs and local delivery. [Transit times can shift depending on your shipping method](https://www.transifly.com/shipping-from-china/).

### What Does It Cost Per Kilogram to Ship from China to America?

Standard air freight costs $3 to $8 per kilogram. Express options run $6 to $12 per kilogram but arrive sooner.

Sea freight charges by container, not weight. One container can fit about 10,000 to 28,000 kilograms, depending on its size.

### Import Duties for Chinese Packages in America: What You Need to Know

Import duties for Chinese packages in America can feel confusing if you’re not used to the process. The rules depend on your product type, value, and whether it’s for personal use or business.

Personal items under $800 usually get through duty-free. But if you’re importing commercial shipments, you’ll deal with a different set of rules and fees.

**Common [duty rates](https://aeblogistics.com/shipping-from-china-to-the-usa-2/) for Chinese packages:**

- Electronics: 0-25%
- Textiles: 10-32%
- Machinery: 0-15%
- Consumer goods: 5-20%
 
You’ll also run into extra fees, like customs brokerage ($50-150) and port handling charges. These can add up fast, especially for bigger shipments.

[Understanding tariff rates](https://www.gerudologistics.com/shipping-from-china-to-us) for Chinese packages helps you budget better. If you’re not sure about your product’s classification, reaching out to a customs broker is honestly the easiest way to get it sorted.
