### Calculating Chargeable and Volumetric Weight

China to USA freight forwarders charge for whichever is greater: actual weight or volumetric weight. This policy prevents carriers from losing out on lightweight but bulky shipments.

Your chargeable weight is what sets your final shipping cost. Calculate volumetric weight by multiplying length × width × height in centimeters, then dividing by a specific factor.

For air freight, divide by 6,000. Express couriers often use 5,000. For example, a box measuring 50cm × 40cm × 30cm equals 60,000 cubic cm, divided by 6,000 = 10 kg volumetric weight.

If that box actually weighs 5 kg, you’re billed for 10 kg. If it’s 15 kg, you pay for 15 kg. Ocean LCL usually charges by cubic meter unless your cargo is unusually dense.

**Volumetric weight formula:**

- **(Length × Width × Height) ÷ Dimensional Factor = Volumetric Weight**
 
Optimizing your packaging can help cut down on wasted space and lower your volumetric charges. Sometimes just a few centimeters make a big difference.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Importers often have questions about working with a China to USA freight forwarder. Here are some common ones, plus a few tips to help you along the way.

### How does live shipment tracking work for freight moving from China to the United States?

Live tracking assigns a unique number to your shipment, which you enter on the carrier’s website or your forwarder’s portal. The system shows real-time updates as your cargo moves through each checkpoint.

[Express couriers issue tracking numbers](https://www.exfreight.com/tracking-shipments-to-the-usa-from-china-a-simple-guide/) at pickup and email them to you. You can check these numbers on DHL, FedEx, or UPS websites to follow your shipment from start to finish.

Air freight uses two types of waybills. The Master Air Waybill tracks the shipment between airports, while the House Air Waybill covers your specific cargo inside a consolidated load.

Ocean freight tracking relies on the Master Bill of Lading from the shipping line and the container number. You enter these on carrier sites like Maersk, MSC, or COSCO to see vessel movements and port arrivals.

### Which carriers and tracking numbers can be used to track deliveries that hand off to USPS in the U.S.?

China Post shipments use 13-character tracking numbers ending in CN. The prefix tells you the service type: RR for registered air mail, LX for ePacket, and EE for EMS.

You can track these numbers on 17track.net or the China Post site while the package is still in China. After it enters the United States, USPS handles the final delivery to your address.

Once you see “Item arrived at USPS facility” in the tracking, switch to usps.com for updates. The same China Post tracking number works on the USPS site for last-mile tracking.

### What factors most affect the total shipping cost from China to the U.S., including price per kilogram?

Your shipping method creates the biggest cost difference. [Express courier services take 3-7 days](https://www.transifly.com/shipping-from-china/) but cost more per kilogram than air or ocean freight.

Cargo weight and volume both matter. Carriers charge based on whichever creates a higher billable weight, so it pays to know the formula.

The origin and destination cities also affect pricing. Shipping from major ports like Shanghai or Shenzhen to Los Angeles or Long Beach costs less than smaller or inland routes.

Fuel surcharges, peak season rates, and customs duties add to the base freight cost. Ocean freight includes terminal handling and container fees, which vary by port.

### How long does shipping from China to the U.S. typically take by air versus ocean freight?

Air freight usually takes 5 to 10 days door-to-door from China to the United States. This covers pickup, airport processing, flight time, customs clearance, and delivery.

Ocean freight takes much longer. Full container loads need 18 to 44 days, depending on whether you ship to the East or West Coast.

Less-than-container shipments require 22 to 45 days because they need extra time for consolidation and deconsolidation. West Coast ports like Los Angeles see vessels arrive faster than East Coast ones.

Express couriers offer the fastest option at 2 to 7 days. These services combine air transport with faster customs clearance and strong ground networks.

### What documents and customs steps are required to avoid delays when importing goods into the United States?

Every shipment needs a commercial invoice with specific details. Make sure to include the Harmonized Tariff Schedule code, declared value, country of origin, and full product descriptions.

Ocean cargo needs an Importer Security Filing (ISF 10+2), submitted at least 24 hours before the vessel leaves China. Missing this filing brings holds and penalties from U.S. Customs and Border Protection.

Regulated products need extra approvals. The FDA regulates food, drugs, and medical devices, while the USDA controls agricultural and animal products.

Your customs broker handles entry filing and duty payment. [Providing accurate documentation upfront](https://www.exfreight.com/tracking-shipments-to-the-usa-from-china-a-simple-guide/) prevents 5 to 14 day holds that happen when CBP finds errors or missing info.
