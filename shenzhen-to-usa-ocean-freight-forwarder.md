## How Much Does Shenzhen-to-USA Ocean Shipping Cost?

Ocean freight costs from Shenzhen depend on container type, cargo volume, the season, and how much of the door-to-door chain you want us to handle. **A useful starting point is to treat the freight quote and landed cost as two different numbers:** the freight quote covers transport, while landed cost also includes duties, taxes, and destination charges.

Sea freight rates shift with fuel surcharges, port congestion, and demand around peak shipping months. A quote from October can look different by December. That’s normal, and it’s why we recommend requesting a fresh quote close to your cargo-ready date instead of relying on an estimate from months earlier.

It’s worth including cargo insurance in your quote request, especially for higher-value electronics or fragile goods commonly shipped from Shenzhen. It’s a smaller cost next to freight and duties, and it can protect against loss or damage during a multi-week ocean transit.

### What Should a Door-to-Door Freight Quote Include?

A complete door-to-door quote should separate five categories: origin pickup, ocean freight, U.S. customs clearance, destination handling, and final-mile delivery. Some shipments use DDP shipping terms, which include estimated duties and taxes; with DDU/DAP, the importer pays duties separately at the time of entry.

We itemize these parts in our quotes rather than giving customers one bundled number. That makes it easier to compare a competing quote line by line and spot gaps hidden behind a low headline price, such as missing destination handling or drayage.

### How Do FCL, LCL, Duties, and Delivery Affect Landed Cost?

FCL pricing is set per container, so your per-unit cost drops as you fill more of it. LCL is priced per cubic meter or per kilogram, whichever is greater. It costs more per unit, but lets you ship smaller volumes without waiting to fill a container.

Duties depend on your HS code and declared cargo value, not the freight cost. Two shipments with identical freight quotes can have very different duty bills if their goods fall under different tariff classifications.

Final-mile delivery adds its own costs. A dock delivery to a business address costs less than a residential delivery or a location that needs liftgate service. Liftgate service carries an additional stated fee of USD 100 when no loading dock is available.

## Which Ocean Service Fits Your Cargo and Deadline?

Choosing the right ocean service for Shenzhen-to-USA cargo comes down to two questions: how much volume are you moving, and how much flexibility does your deadline allow? **FCL suits shipments large enough to fill or nearly fill a container, while LCL works for smaller loads that don’t justify booking a whole box.** Air freight and express courier are faster alternatives when ocean transit doesn’t fit your schedule.

China to USA shipping options fall into three core speed tiers. Ocean freight remains the most cost-effective method for commercial volume.

Air freight cuts weeks down to days, though costs are higher per kilogram. Express courier works best for urgent product samples rather than bulk inventory.

### FCL for Larger Commercial Shipments

FCL, or Full Container Load, gives you a dedicated 20-foot, 40-foot, or 40-foot high-cube container. Nobody else’s cargo shares the box, which means less handling and a lower risk of damage or mix-ups.

We handle FCL bookings for larger shipments, cargo that needs extra security, and businesses looking to lower per-unit shipping costs. FCL also supports multi-supplier consolidation: cargo from more than one Shenzhen supplier can go into a single container before departure.

### LCL and Multi-Supplier Consolidation for Smaller Loads

LCL, or Less than Container Load, works for businesses that don’t have enough cargo to fill a container on their own. We consolidate smaller shipments into a shared container, so you pay only for the space your cargo uses.

This option suits new product launches, test orders, and restocks between larger FCL shipments. LCL door-to-door service includes customs clearance coordination, warehousing and distribution, and delivery to Amazon FBA.

### What Transit Time Should You Plan for from Shenzhen?

Plan for door-to-door ocean freight from Shenzhen to the USA to take about 30 to 40 days. That estimate includes factory pickup, port-to-port transit, import clearance, and final delivery, not just the time the vessel spends at sea.

Faster options are available when your schedule is tight. Standard air freight generally takes about 8 to 10 days, while express courier takes about 3 to 5 days. Both cost more per kilogram than ocean freight, so most commercial-volume shipments still move by sea.

Customs processing, port congestion, weather, carrier schedules, and seasonal demand can all affect transit time. Build a buffer into your inventory planning, especially before peak shipping season, to keep a short delay from turning into a stockout.

### U.S. Customs Entry, Importer Responsibilities, and Bond Options

Customs clearance coordination is part of our door-to-door service. It typically costs around USD 125, depending on the commodities in the shipment.

Managing US customs clearance involves classification, documentation review, and formal entry filing. While direct-to-consumer parcels may enter under the de minimis exemption, commercial ocean cargo requires formal entry regardless of shipment value.

The importer remains responsible for duties and taxes. That holds true for Amazon FBA: Amazon does not act as Importer of Record and rejects shipments with unpaid fees.

Securing a valid US customs bond is required before commercial cargo can clear. For customs bond needs, we can arrange an annual bond or offer the use of an AEB bond on a case-by-case basis.

We require a Power of Attorney whenever AEB Logistics coordinates customs entry. We work directly with licensed customs brokerage partners rather than acting as the broker ourselves.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does door-to-door ocean freight from Shenzhen to the USA take?

Door-to-door ocean freight from Shenzhen generally takes about 30 to 40 days, including pickup, transit, customs, and final delivery. Port congestion, customs holds, and peak-season demand can extend that window, so we recommend adding a buffer for time-sensitive inventory.

### Is LCL or FCL cheaper for my Shenzhen shipment?

FCL costs less per unit once you have enough cargo to fill most of a container. You pay a flat container rate, no matter how full it is. LCL costs less overall for small shipments because you pay only for the cubic meters or kilograms your cargo uses.
