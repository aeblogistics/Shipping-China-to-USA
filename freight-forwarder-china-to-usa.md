### How Do You Figure Out Cost Per Kilogram for Air Shipping?

Shipping from China to US? You’ll want to nail down your cost per kilogram before you book a flight for your goods. This is crucial whether you’re using air freight, sea freight, or any other way to ship from China to US.

**Step 1:** First, get your cargo’s actual weight in kilograms.

**Step 2:** Next, calculate volumetric weight. Use this formula:

(Length × Width × Height in cm) ÷ 6,000 = Volumetric weight in kg

**Step 3:** Airlines charge you based on whichever weight is higher.

**Example calculation:**

- Let’s say your box measures 50cm × 40cm × 30cm.
- Volumetric weight: (50 × 40 × 30) ÷ 6,000 = 10 kg.
- Actual weight: 8 kg.
- **Chargeable weight:** That’s 10 kg, since it’s the higher number.

If your rate is $5 per kg, total shipping cost is $50. Don’t forget to add fuel surcharges, security fees, and handling charges—those can sneak up on you.
