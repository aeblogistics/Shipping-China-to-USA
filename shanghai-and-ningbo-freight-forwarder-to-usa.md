## Choose Your Air, Ocean, or Amazon FBA Service

The right shipping mode depends on your cargo volume, deadline, and whether the destination is a warehouse or an Amazon fulfillment center. International freight forwarding requires balancing cost against speed across every leg of the journey.

We coordinate ocean freight, air freight, and Amazon FBA forwarding as part of one freight forwarding services package, so your choice of mode doesn’t change who you work with.

Sea freight remains the standard choice for planned inventory restocks. Air freight and express courier options help when a shipment can’t wait weeks for ocean transit. For e-commerce logistics and e-commerce fulfillment operations feeding Amazon FBA, the delivery method also needs to account for warehousing and distribution at the destination, not just the ocean or air leg.

### FCL Shipping for Dedicated Container Space

FCL shipping gives your cargo a dedicated container, available in 20-foot, 40-foot, and 40-foot high-cube sizes. It suits larger shipments, cargo that needs extra security, or importers looking to lower their per-unit shipping cost.

We coordinate factory pickup, container booking, customs clearance, and door-to-door delivery for FCL shipments from Shanghai or Ningbo. Because the container stays closed during transit instead of being opened for consolidation, FCL shipments generally pass through fewer handling points than shared-container freight.

### LCL Shipping and Multi-Supplier Consolidation

LCL shipping suits businesses that don’t have enough cargo to fill a full container. We consolidate smaller shipments into shared containers, so you pay for the space your cargo actually uses rather than an entire box.

This option works well when combining goods from multiple suppliers around Shanghai and Ningbo into one shipment. We provide flexible options like free warehousing at origin to help consolidate orders before loading.

LCL includes door-to-door shipping, customs clearance coordination, and warehousing and distribution once the shipment reaches the destination country.

### Air Freight and Express Options for Urgent Cargo

Air freight moves faster than ocean freight but costs more per kilogram. It works well for time-sensitive, high-value, or relatively small shipments, especially when a product launch or restock deadline can’t wait weeks for ocean transit.

For urgent parcel express, air cargo works alongside courier networks like DHL, FedEx, and UPS to guarantee fast international shipping.

As a reference point, an air shipment from Hong Kong to Los Angeles can take around five days, while ocean freight on a comparable route takes at least three weeks. General guidance for Amazon FBA shipments puts air freight lead times at about 7 to 10 days, depending on the shipment and destination.

### What Determines Your Freight Rate and Landed Cost?

Cargo volume and weight are the first factors that shape your freight rate. Full container shipments generally cost less per unit than partial loads, while LCL cargo is priced by the cubic meters (CBM) it occupies.

Landed cost combines the product price, freight charges, customs duties, tariffs, and final delivery into one per-unit figure. **Skipping this calculation is how importers get surprised at the invoice stage**, because a low freight quote can still lead to a high landed cost once duties and destination charges are added.

## Routes and Transit Times from Shanghai and Ningbo

Shanghai and Ningbo both offer weekly sailings to major US gateways, but the right origin depends on your supplier’s location and which US coast your cargo needs to reach. Choosing between the ports and between West Coast and East Coast delivery affects both cost and schedule reliability.

### Choosing an Origin Based on Your Supplier and Cargo

Shanghai and Ningbo both serve the Yangtze River Delta manufacturing base, but suppliers cluster differently around each port. Shenzhen and Guangzhou connect to Pearl River Delta electronics and apparel producers, while Qingdao supports machinery and goods exporters in the Bohai Rim farther north.

Buyers sourcing through Alibaba sometimes work with suppliers across several of these regions for the same shipment. We coordinate factory pickup near Shanghai, Ningbo, Shenzhen, Guangzhou, and Qingdao, so cargo from multiple supplier locations can fit into one shipment plan.

### West Coast, East Coast, and Inland US Delivery

Ocean freight from Shanghai or Ningbo typically routes through West Coast gateways such as Los Angeles and Long Beach for faster trans-Pacific transit. For accelerated ocean transit, expedited services like Matson Express offer fast port calls with rapid container discharge.

East Coast ports like New York/New Jersey make more sense when the final destination is closer to the Eastern Seaboard, though you’ll trade off some ocean transit time.

Houston and Chicago offer additional gateway and inland options, depending on where your distribution network sits. We coordinate inland trucking from the port that receives your cargo and move it on to the final warehouse or FBA address.

### How Long Do Air, Express, and Ocean Shipments Take?

Managing transit times for shipping from China to the USA requires looking at total door-to-door movement. Ocean freight from China to the USA generally takes about 30 to 40 days door to door.

Standard air freight takes about 8 to 10 days, and express courier service takes about 3 to 5 days. Actual transit times vary with customs processing, port congestion, weather, carrier schedules, and seasonal demand.

A shipment headed to a West Coast port typically clears faster than one routed to the East Coast because the ocean leg is shorter. Build in buffer time around these estimates rather than treating them as fixed delivery dates.

### Documents and Import Requirements to Prepare

Import clearance depends on accurate paperwork prepared before the shipment leaves China. The core documents include the commercial invoice, packing list, and bill of lading, along with the correct HS code classification for the goods.

Getting the HS code classification right matters for more than paperwork. It determines duty rates and whether Section 301 tariffs apply to the shipment, so errors can delay clearance or trigger unexpected charges at the border.

### Customs Brokerage, Bonds, and Importer Responsibilities

We coordinate customs brokerage as part of the shipment, including HS/HTS code guidance, import-entry documentation support, and customs bond options. Partnering with a licensed customs broker ensures your filings clear US Customs without unnecessary hold-ups.

For US imports, we can arrange an annual customs bond or offer use of an AEB bond on a case-by-case basis.

You need to provide a Power of Attorney if you ask us to perform customs clearance on your behalf. Importers remain responsible for duties and taxes, regardless of who files the entry. For Amazon FBA shipments specifically, Amazon doesn’t act as Importer of Record and won’t accept cargo that arrives with unpaid duties, taxes, or shipping charges.
