## Choose the Right Ports and Container Service for Your Delivery

**The right port pair gets your cargo to its final address at the lowest total cost, not necessarily by the shortest ocean crossing.** Selecting the most efficient shipping routes from China to the USA depends on cargo volume and final destination. A West Coast arrival can mean a long rail or truck leg, while an all-water route to the East Coast takes longer at sea but may shorten the inland trip.

### Pickup and Export Through Shanghai, Ningbo, Shenzhen, and Qingdao

We coordinate supplier pickup near Shanghai, Ningbo, Shenzhen, and Qingdao. Shanghai and Ningbo serve the Yangtze River Delta factory base, while Shenzhen serves Guangdong’s electronics and apparel makers. Qingdao serves machinery and goods exporters in the north.

Choose your origin port based on supplier location, cargo-ready date, and sailing schedule. A slightly longer truck run in China to reach a port with better sailings can be the smarter choice.

### West Coast Delivery Through Los Angeles, Long Beach, and Seattle

The Port of Los Angeles and Port of Long Beach are the primary trans-Pacific gateways for cargo headed to California, the western states, and inland markets by rail or truck. Seattle works well for the Pacific Northwest.

Los Angeles and Long Beach process the highest container volumes from Asia. We partner with top ocean carriers like Maersk, COSCO, CMA CGM, and Hapag-Lloyd to secure consistent space allocations.

These ports offer the shortest ocean crossing. Before choosing one, check the distance from the port to your delivery ZIP code, since that leg adds cost and time.

### East Coast and Gulf Coast Delivery Through New York, Savannah, and Houston

Cargo for the eastern United States can cross the Pacific and pass through the Panama Canal to New York and New Jersey or the Port of Savannah. Houston serves Texas and the South. The sea voyage takes longer, but inland transportation can be much shorter and less expensive.

The Gulf Coast isn’t automatically cheaper or faster. Compare vessel schedules, destination port charges, and trucking or rail costs from the port.

### FCL, LCL, and Multi-Supplier Consolidation Options

Full container load (FCL) gives your cargo its own dedicated container in 20-foot, 40-foot, or 40-foot high-cube sizes. It suits larger volumes, cargo that needs extra security, and importers looking for a lower cost per unit.

Less than container load (LCL) puts your goods in a shared container, so you pay only for the space you use. If you buy from several factories, we can combine cargo from multiple suppliers into one consolidated shipment when it makes sense. ## How Much Will Your Container Shipment Cost?

### What Is Included in Port-to-Port and Door-to-Door Pricing?

Port-to-port pricing covers the ocean leg and port handling. Door-to-door pricing adds factory pickup, export handling, customs coordination, destination handling, and inland delivery. A low port-to-port price may leave out charges that show up later.

A complete quote itemizes:

- Pickup and export handling in China
- Ocean freight
- Customs brokerage, which can be included and usually costs around USD 125, depending on the number of commodities
- Destination handling and inland delivery
- Extras such as liftgate service at USD 100, or residential delivery fees
 

We can provide an itemized charge breakdown on request. See our [door-to-door shipping costs from China to USA](https://aeblogistics.com/door-to-door-shipping-from-china-to-usa-costs/) page for more detail.

### How Duties, Tariffs, and Destination Charges Affect Landed Cost

Landed cost includes the product price, freight, duties, tariffs, fees, and delivery. Your HTS code and cargo value determine duties. Depending on the product, Section 301 tariffs, anti-dumping duties, and other measures may apply, along with the merchandise processing fee and harbor maintenance fee.

Tariff rules change often, so confirm what applies to your product before shipping. The importer remains responsible for duties and taxes. Amazon doesn’t act as Importer of Record and won’t accept shipments that arrive with unpaid duties, taxes, or shipping charges.

With DDP shipping, duties and taxes are settled before arrival. Confirm in writing what a DDP quote includes and excludes. Our [DDP shipping](https://aeblogistics.com/ddp-shipping/) page explains the responsibilities.

### When Do Volume, Season, and Routing Change Freight Rates?

Rates rise ahead of retail peaks and Lunar New Year, when peak season surcharges may apply. Full containers cost less per unit than partial loads. East Coast routes add ocean time, while West Coast routes add inland distance.

Book early if your cargo-ready date falls during a busy period. Quotes have an expiry date, so check it before planning around a price. ### Prepare Import Documents and Coordinate US Customs Clearance

You’ll need a commercial invoice, a packing list, and a bill of lading, or an air waybill if moving expedited goods. You must file an importer security filing (ISF) before ocean cargo is loaded for the USA, and some products need a certificate of origin or FCC certification. The HTS code on your invoice drives the duty rate, so classify it carefully.

Smooth US customs clearance requires precise documentation. Any missing paperwork can delay release at destination terminals.

We need a Power of Attorney if you ask us to coordinate customs clearance. We offer customs bond options, including an annual bond or use of an AEB bond in applicable cases. The importer remains responsible for duties and taxes. Learn more about [customs brokerage](https://aeblogistics.com/customs-brokerage/) support.

### Track Milestones and Manage Transit Disruptions

You can track your shipment through Accepted, In transit, Export Customs, Export Departure, Import Entry, Import Arrival, and Delivered. Port congestion, blank sailings, and transshipment can shift schedules, and your account manager flags changes as they appear.

We can arrange cargo insurance if you request it. It’s worth considering for higher-value loads.

### Arrange Drayage, Warehousing, and Amazon FBA Delivery

After release, we coordinate container drayage and inland transportation through carrier partners, including Schneider, J.B. Hunt, YRC, Landstar, North American, UPS Freight, and FedEx Freight. We work with these carriers but don’t run our own trucks.

For Amazon sellers, we handle FBA labeling, palletization, and delivery appointments through Amazon’s Carrier Appointment Request Portal. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to ship a 40-foot container from China to the USA?

The cost depends on the origin and destination ports, the season, and how much of the journey your quote covers. Market estimates vary widely, so we quote live rates rather than a fixed figure. Send us your shipment details, and we’ll itemize ocean freight, handling, customs, and delivery.

### Which Chinese and US ports can AEB Logistics arrange shipping through?

We coordinate pickup and export through Shanghai, Ningbo, Shenzhen, and Qingdao. We deliver through Los Angeles, Long Beach, Seattle, New York and New Jersey, Savannah, and Houston. Other routes may be available, depending on your shipment and routing. Tell us your supplier and delivery locations, and we’ll recommend a port pair.

### How long does container shipping from China to the USA take?

Our published estimate for door-to-door ocean freight is about 30 to 40 days. Port-to-port sailing takes less time, so compare quotes on the same basis. Customs processing, port congestion, weather, carrier schedules, and the season can all affect timing.

### Should I choose a West Coast port or an all-water East Coast route?

Choose based on your final delivery address. West Coast ports mean a shorter ocean crossing, which works well for western and many inland destinations. An all-water route to New York or Savannah takes longer at sea but can shorten the inland haul for eastern deliveries. Compare the full journey to see which works best.

### Can AEB Logistics combine goods from multiple suppliers into one container?

Yes, we can consolidate cargo from multiple suppliers when it makes sense. This can lower per-unit freight costs compared with shipping each supplier’s goods separately. Share each supplier’s location and cargo-ready date so we can plan the shipment.

### What is the fastest alternative if my cargo cannot wait for ocean freight?

Air freight from China to the USA is the fastest alternative, and express courier is faster still for small parcels. We estimate about 8 to 10 days for standard air freight and 3 to 5 days for express courier.

While air freight costs are higher than ocean shipping, it significantly shortens lead times for urgent inventory. Air rates are based on chargeable weight, which is whichever is greater: actual or volumetric weight.
