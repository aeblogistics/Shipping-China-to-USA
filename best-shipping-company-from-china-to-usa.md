## Shipping Methods and Service Types for Best Shipping Company from China to USA

When you’re shipping from China to USA, you have a few main choices. Each has its own pros and cons for speed and price. [Sea freight is still the go-to for big shipments](https://abl-logistics.com/a-comprehensive-comparison-of-shipping-methods-from-china-to-usa/), but air freight and express couriers are great when time is tight. Rail is an option for some routes, though not as common.

### Sea Freight: FCL and LCL Options with the Best Shipping Company from China to USA

Sea freight is usually the cheapest way to move [bulk goods](https://aeblogistics.com/cargo-from-china-to-usa/) from China to the USA. You’ve got two main choices, depending on how much you’re shipping.

**FCL (Full Container Load)** means you use a whole container just for your stuff. It’s best if you’ve got enough to fill a 20- or 40-foot container. FCL usually costs $1,000–$4,000 per container, with transit times around 20–40 days.

**LCL (Less than Container Load)** is for smaller shipments. You share space with others and pay by the cubic meter, usually $100–$300 per CBM. It’s flexible for businesses that don’t need a full container.

FCL tends to be more secure since it’s just your goods. LCL is more flexible, but your cargo gets mixed with others, which can mean more handling.

### Air Freight and Express Courier Services with the Best Shipping Company from China to USA

Air freight gets your goods delivered in 3–7 days. It’s fast, but you’ll pay more—typically $5–$10 per kilogram. It’s great for valuable or urgent shipments.

Express couriers like DHL, FedEx, and UPS can deliver in 1–5 days. They handle everything, including customs and door-to-door service. Express shipping usually costs $20–$50 per kilogram, depending on size and speed.

So, what’s the difference? Express couriers are faster and offer full tracking, but they’re pricier. Air freight is a middle ground for speed and cost.

- **Speed:** Express couriers are fastest
- **Handling:** Door-to-door service
- **Tracking:** Detailed updates
- **Cost:** Express is most expensive

Pick air freight for urgent, medium-sized shipments. Go with express couriers for small packages that need to arrive yesterday.

### Door-to-Door and DDP Shipping Solutions from the Best Shipping Company from China to USA

Door-to-door shipping means your shipment goes from your supplier in China right to your US address. The company handles pickup, shipping, customs, and final delivery.

**DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)** means the shipping company covers everything—duties, taxes, customs fees. You get one price, with no surprises when your shipment arrives.

These services are great if you don’t want to deal with logistics or paperwork. Your [freight forwarder](https://aeblogistics.com/freight-forwarder-in-china/) takes care of it all, so you have one point of contact and less hassle.

### Rail and Road Freight Alternatives for Shipping from China to USA

Rail freight can be a middle ground between sea and air for some routes. It’s not common for China to USA, but where available, it can be faster than ocean and cheaper than air.

Road freight is mostly for moving goods inside China to ports or airports, and for final delivery in the USA after customs. It’s crucial for the last stretch of your shipment.

Sometimes, the best shipping company from China to USA will use a mix of truck, ship, and plane to get your goods where they need to go. It’s all about finding the right balance of speed and price.

## Customs Clearance and Regulatory Compliance

Shipping with AEB Logistics from China to the USA means you’ll need to handle customs clearance and follow import regulations. Understanding the right documents, shipping terms, and how customs brokers work can help you sidestep delays and extra fees.

### Customs Procedures and Documentation for AEB Logistics

When your shipment lands at a U.S. port, it has to clear [customs clearance from China to the USA](https://topshipping.co/customs-clearance-from-china-to-the-usa/). You’ll need to submit the required paperwork to U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP).

Four documents are essential for importing: the commercial invoice, packing list, Bill of Lading (or Airway Bill), and Certificate of Origin. The commercial invoice lists your goods, quantities, values, and payment terms.

The packing list breaks down how items are packed, including weights and sizes. Your Bill of Lading or Airway Bill shows proof of shipment and ownership, while the Certificate of Origin proves where your goods were made.

CBP checks these documents to calculate duties and confirm compliance. If something’s missing or wrong, you’ll face delays or even fines. AEB Logistics can submit documents through the Automated Commercial Environment (ACE) system to speed things up.

Customs might inspect your shipment, which can add 1-3 days to your timeline. Sometimes they pick shipments at random, or they target certain products for a closer look.

### Incoterms and Shipping Terms: CIF, FOB, DDP with AEB Logistics

Incoterms spell out who pays for shipping and who’s on the hook if something goes wrong. Shipping from China with AEB Logistics, you’ll see three common terms.

**FOB (Free On Board)** means the seller delivers goods to the departure port and loads them onto the vessel. You pay for ocean freight, insurance, and everything after loading. You’re also responsible for customs clearance and duties in the USA.

**CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight)** means the seller covers shipping and insurance to the destination port. You still pay import duties and clear customs. The seller’s risk ends once goods reach the U.S. port.

**DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)** puts all the responsibility on the seller, including shipping, insurance, customs clearance, and import duties. Your goods arrive ready to go with no customs headaches on your end.

FOB is best if you want control over costs and forwarders. Go with DDP if you want things as easy as possible.

### Role of Customs Brokerage in Importing with AEB Logistics

[Customs brokerage](https://winsaillogistics.com/top-freight-forwarders-for-usa-imports-from-china/) is a game-changer for U.S. import clearance, tariff classification, and making sure you’re following the rules. Licensed brokers handle entry documents, classify products with Harmonized System (HS) codes, and figure out the right duty rates.

Brokers calculate fees like the Merchandise Processing Fee and Harbor Maintenance Fee. They talk directly to CBP to sort out any issues with your shipment. AEB Logistics either has licensed brokers on staff or partners with [customs brokerage](https://aeblogistics.com/customs-brokerage/) firms.

Using a broker means fewer mistakes in your paperwork and product codes. They keep up with changing import rules and trade policies, so you don’t get caught off guard. This kind of expertise can save you from penalties and speed up your global trade process.
