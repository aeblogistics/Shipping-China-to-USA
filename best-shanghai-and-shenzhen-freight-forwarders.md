Shanghai handles a huge share of container and air cargo bound for the US, while Shenzhen is next to some of the country’s busiest electronics and hardware factories. Evaluating freight forwarders in China requires understanding the difference between general cargo movers and Amazon FBA shipping specialists. Agile partners often deliver better results for e-commerce brands than rigid enterprise networks.

     The right shipping agent gives you a clear, itemized quote and tells you exactly who handles US customs entry. Directory listings and search rankings can help you find candidates, but they don’t tell you which forwarder will handle your specific product and shipping lane smoothly. Run each finalist through these checks before booking.

### Can the agent coordinate pickup from your supplier’s actual location?

Ask which exact cities or districts the agent covers for factory pickup, not just the province. A Shenzhen-based forwarder that covers Yantian and Shekou ports may not also pick up in Dongguan or Guangzhou without charging extra for trucking.

If you source from multiple factories, ask whether the forwarder consolidates cargo at its own warehouse before export. That affects both your costs and how many separate pickups you need to schedule.

### Does the quote distinguish freight, customs charges, duties, and final delivery?

A reliable quote lists ocean or air freight, origin and destination handling fees, customs brokerage, estimated duties, and final-mile delivery as separate items. If a quote bundles everything into one number, it’s hard to see where costs might change once your cargo ships.

Ask each forwarder for an itemized breakdown based on the same cargo details: the same HS code and cargo value, and the same Incoterm, whether FOB or EXW.

### How does the agent handle Amazon FBA labeling and delivery appointments?

FBA-ready shipments need correct labeling, sometimes UPS labeling, and a delivery appointment scheduled through Amazon’s Carrier Appointment Request Portal. Ask whether the forwarder books the appointment directly or needs you to submit your own bill of lading and FBA booking form so it can coordinate it.

For LCL shipments, check whether the forwarder schedules appointments only after your cargo arrives at its destination-country warehouse. That step adds time to the total delivery window.

### What tracking and exception support will you receive?

Ask which shipment milestones you’ll see and how quickly the forwarder flags delays, customs holds, or documentation issues. Useful tracking systems show export customs clearance, export departure, import entry, import arrival, and final delivery, so you can see where your cargo is.

Check whether you’ll have one dedicated contact for questions or be routed through a general support queue while your shipment is in transit.

## How to Compare China-to-USA Shipping Quotes

Compare China-to-USA quotes only when every forwarder is pricing the exact same shipment details. Even small differences in the information you submit, such as an inaccurate cargo-ready date or a missing HS code, can make one quote look cheaper than it will be once the actual charges come in.

### Which cargo details should you send every forwarder?

Send every forwarder the same origin and destination, Incoterm (FOB or EXW), HS code, cargo value, box count, cubic meters, weight in kilograms, and estimated cargo-ready date. Missing any of these details is a common reason quotes come back inconsistent or lower than the final invoice.

Include any special requirements, such as a target delivery date or FBA warehouse split, so each quote covers the same work.

Ensure that your commercial invoice and packing list match your initial inquiry. Discrepancies in customs documentation routinely cause costly clearance delays and tariff audits at destination ports.

### When should you compare air freight, LCL, and FCL?

Compare air freight when your shipment is time-sensitive, high-value, or small enough that speed is worth the higher per-kilogram cost. Air freight from Hong Kong to Los Angeles can take around five days, while ocean freight on the same route can take at least three weeks.

Compare LCL when your cargo won’t fill a container and you want to pay only for the space you use. Compare FCL when you have enough volume to fill a 20-foot, 40-foot, or 40-foot high-cube container and want a lower per-unit cost. Published estimates put standard air freight from China to the USA at around 8 to 10 days and door-to-door ocean freight at around 30 to 40 days, though customs processing, port congestion, and carrier schedules can affect transit times.

### Who will act as Importer of Record and pay US import duties?

As the importer, you remain responsible for duties and taxes regardless of which forwarder you use. Amazon doesn’t act as Importer of Record for FBA shipments and won’t accept cargo with unpaid duties, taxes, or shipping charges. Before shipping, confirm whether your forwarder pays duties upfront or bills you separately after clearance.

Ask whether the forwarder uses its own customs bond or requires you to hold an annual bond. You’ll also need a Power of Attorney whenever you ask a forwarder to clear customs on your behalf.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which Shanghai or Shenzhen freight forwarder is best for Amazon FBA shipments to the USA?

Forwarders that document FBA labeling, delivery appointment scheduling, and warehouse-splitting services are a strong fit for FBA shipments. These tasks directly affect whether Amazon accepts your delivery. AEB Logistics and DFH Global Logistics both publish specific FBA services, including labeling and Amazon delivery coordination, so they’re worth comparing directly for FBA-bound cargo.

### Should I choose a shipping agent in Shanghai or Shenzhen?

Choose based on your supplier’s location, not the city’s general reputation. Shanghai works well for sellers sourcing from Zhejiang and Jiangsu-area factories, with strong port and Pudong airport access. Shenzhen suits sellers sourcing electronics and hardware from South China’s manufacturing belt near Yantian and Shekou ports.

### Can these forwarders collect goods from multiple Chinese suppliers?

Many forwarders offer consolidated shipping, combining cargo from multiple suppliers into one shipment and reducing the number of separate international bookings you need. Confirm each forwarder’s consolidation process and warehouse location, as pickup coverage and free storage periods vary by provider.

### How do I compare DDP quotes from China-to-USA freight forwarders?

Compare DDP, or Delivered Duty Paid, quotes by confirming that each provider includes export declaration, international transport, import customs clearance, duty and tax payment, and final delivery in one all-in price. Request an itemized breakdown to see exactly what’s covered and what might be billed separately later.

### Will Amazon accept an FBA shipment with unpaid duties or shipping charges?

No. Amazon isn’t the Importer of Record, so it won’t accept FBA shipments that arrive with unpaid duties, taxes, or shipping charges. Before your cargo leaves China, confirm with your freight forwarder exactly when and how you’ll settle these charges.
