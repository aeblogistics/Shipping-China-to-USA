## How to Choose Between Shanghai and Ningbo Shipping

Your choice between Shanghai and Ningbo-Zhoushan usually depends on your supplier’s location, not which port has more capacity. Both rank among the world’s busiest container ports, and most forwarders that serve one also serve the other.

### Which port is better located for your supplier?

Shanghai suits suppliers in Jiangsu Province and the northern Yangtze Delta. Ningbo-Zhoushan is often closer for suppliers in Zhejiang Province, including manufacturing clusters near Yiwu. Trucking your goods to the nearer port cuts inland transport costs and can shave a day or two off pre-shipment handling.

If your supplier already works with a forwarder at one port, that familiarity often matters more than a small difference in distance. Ask which port the supplier typically uses for exports; factories often favor routes that have worked well for them before.

### How should you compare sailing schedules and door-to-door transit times?

Compare weekly sailing frequency and named carrier services for your specific route, rather than relying on an average transit estimate. AEB Logistics publishes a general door-to-door ocean freight estimate of roughly 30-40 days from China to the USA, while standard air freight takes about 8-10 days.

Treat any transit figure from any forwarder as a route-specific estimate, not a guarantee. Customs processing, port congestion, weather, and carrier schedules all affect actual arrival times. Ask each forwarder for its latest sailing schedule to your destination port and the average time cargo spends at origin before departure. If a forwarder can’t provide a specific sailing schedule, ask more questions before booking.

### When does FCL make more sense than LCL?

FCL makes sense when your cargo fills roughly 60-70% of a 20-foot or 40-foot container. At that point, paying for unused space in LCL consolidation can cost more than booking a dedicated container. Below that threshold, LCL usually costs less because you pay only for the volume you ship.

FCL also reduces handling risk because your goods don’t travel mixed with other shippers’ cargo during transit and consolidation. For time-sensitive or high-value cargo, that lower handling risk may be worth the higher per-unit cost, even below the volume breakeven point.

## How to Compare Total Landed Cost

Comparing quotes by freight rate alone is one of the most common mistakes SMB importers make. Freight often accounts for less than half of your total landed cost. A full comparison includes origin charges, ocean or air freight, destination handling, customs duties, taxes, and final-mile delivery.

### Which origin and destination charges should a quote include?

A complete quote should list factory pickup, export customs clearance, terminal handling at the origin port, ocean or air freight, destination terminal handling, import customs clearance, and final delivery as separate line items. If a quote leaves out any of these, the charge may show up later as an unexpected invoice.

Ask whether liftgate service is included for delivery locations without a loading dock. AEB Logistics says this can cost an additional USD 100 where applicable, and other forwarders may charge similar fees for residential deliveries or accessorial services.

### How do Incoterms change the costs you are responsible for?

Incoterms define where supplier cost responsibility ends and yours begins. Under FOB, your supplier covers charges through loading at Shanghai or Ningbo, leaving you responsible for sea freight, destination handling, and cargo insurance.

Under EXW, you pay starting from the factory floor, including trucking to port facilities. Meanwhile, DDP shipping shifts customs clearance and destination duties back to the forwarder, simplifying your landed cost calculations.

### What customs duties, taxes, and delivery fees should you budget for?

Import duties and taxes depend on your product’s HS/HTS code and declared cargo value. You remain responsible for paying them as the importer of record, no matter which forwarder you use. Once you provide your HS code and cargo value, ask your forwarder to estimate duties and taxes. A reliable upfront estimate can help prevent budget surprises at destination.

Budget separately for final-mile delivery charges, which may include LTL trucking, residential delivery fees, or Amazon FBA delivery appointment costs. For UK-bound cargo, importers may also need to pay Customs Duty and VAT before the goods are released, and they must hold a valid EORI number for customs clearance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best freight forwarder from Shanghai or Ningbo to the USA for a small importer?

A forwarder with a dedicated account manager, both FCL and LCL options, and clear customs brokerage pricing tends to suit a small importer shipping a few containers a year. AEB Logistics offers this service model, with customs brokerage estimated at around USD 125 per shipment.

### Is Shanghai or Ningbo better for shipping goods to the USA?

Neither port is always better; the right choice depends on where your supplier’s factory is located. Shanghai suits suppliers in the northern Yangtze Delta, while Ningbo-Zhoushan is often closer for suppliers in Zhejiang Province and near Yiwu.

### How long does ocean freight from Shanghai or Ningbo to the USA take?

According to AEB Logistics’ general transit estimates, door-to-door ocean freight from China to the USA takes roughly 30-40 days. Actual transit time varies with carrier schedules, port congestion, customs processing, and the season, so treat any published figure as an estimate rather than a guarantee.

### Do these freight forwarders help with US customs clearance?

Most established forwarders, including AEB Logistics, coordinate customs brokerage and support import-entry documentation and HS/HTS code guidance. The forwarder typically needs a Power of Attorney to file customs clearance on your behalf. As the importer of record, you remain responsible for paying any duties and taxes owed.

### How can I fairly compare freight quotes from Shanghai and Ningbo to the USA?

First, confirm that both quotes use the same Incoterm. An FOB quote and an EXW quote cover different costs, so comparing them directly won’t give you a fair picture. Ask for an itemized breakdown of origin charges, freight, destination handling, customs brokerage, and final delivery. That way, you can compare the total landed cost, not just the freight rate.
