     ## How to Compare Door-to-Door Ocean Freight Quotes

To compare quotes, ask every forwarder for the same scope of work, not just the same cargo details. A cheaper-looking quote may leave out a step that another provider has already included.

Many importers look for the cheapest way to ship from China to the USA, but the lowest base quote often excludes destination drayage, chassis fees, or demurrage risk. Evaluating the complete door-to-door scope ensures you avoid hidden expenses.

### Which charges are included from Shenzhen pickup through US delivery?

Ask each forwarder to list factory pickup, export customs, ocean freight, US customs brokerage, duties, and final delivery as separate charges. When a quote bundles everything into one number, it’s hard to see which provider actually costs less once you account for every charge.

### Who coordinates customs entry, and who pays duties and taxes?

Check whether the forwarder files the customs entry or uses a separate licensed broker, and ask in writing who holds the customs bond. Duties and taxes are still the importer’s responsibility, no matter who files the paperwork, so budget for them separately from the freight quote.

### Does FCL or LCL better fit your cargo volume and delivery needs?

FCL makes sense when your cargo fills enough space to justify a dedicated 20-foot, 40-foot, or 40-foot high-cube container. LCL combines smaller loads in shared container space, so you pay only for the space you use. If you ship several smaller orders regularly, ask whether your [LCL and consolidated shipping](https://aeblogistics.com/sea-freight-from-china-to-usa/) option includes warehousing and distribution at the destination.

### What shipment details should you provide for comparable quotes?

Give every forwarder the same details: origin and destination, Incoterms like FOB or EXW, HS code, cargo value, box count, cubic meters and kilograms, and your estimated cargo-ready date. Different details in quote requests are the most common reason two forwarders’ prices seem impossible to compare side by side.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which Shenzhen freight forwarder is best for door-to-door shipping to the USA?

The best choice depends on your cargo volume and Amazon exposure. Importers who want one contact to manage everything from pickup through delivery may find forwarders like AEB Logistics useful for Amazon FBA coordination and customs documentation. Larger industrial shippers may prefer a provider with more corporate infrastructure, such as AIT Worldwide Logistics or SEKO Logistics.

### How much does ocean shipping from Shenzhen to the USA cost?

Ocean freight prices vary with container size, cargo volume, and current carrier rates, so there’s no single price for every shipment. Ask for an itemized quote that separates freight, customs brokerage (often around USD 125 for US customs coordination), duties, and delivery. That way, you can compare the full landed cost instead of one freight figure.

### How long does door-to-door ocean shipping from Shenzhen to the USA take?

Door-to-door ocean freight from China to the USA generally takes around 30 to 40 days, including export processing, transit, US customs, and final delivery. Port congestion, customs holds, and seasonal demand can add time, so leave a buffer when planning inventory.

### Do Shenzhen freight forwarders handle US customs clearance?

Most established Shenzhen forwarders coordinate US customs clearance themselves or through a licensed customs broker. They typically include it as a line item in the shipping quote. Before a forwarder can file entry documents for you, they usually need a Power of Attorney. The importer still has to pay duties and taxes, regardless of who files.

### What should US importers check before booking a Shenzhen shipping agent?

Confirm that the agent can pick up from your supplier’s address, whether the quote itemizes costs or bundles them, and who handles US customs entry. If your cargo is headed to an Amazon fulfillment center, check whether the agent supports FBA delivery appointments. Not every Shenzhen forwarder includes that in its standard service.
