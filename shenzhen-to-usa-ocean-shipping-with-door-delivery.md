## Choose the Right Ocean Freight Service for Your Cargo

 **The right ocean freight service for a Shenzhen shipment comes down to cargo volume: full containers for larger loads, shared containers for smaller ones.** Sea freight from Shenzhen to the USA moves through FCL (Full Container Load) or LCL (Less than Container Load), and each works for a different shipment profile.

### FCL Shipping for Dedicated Container Space

 FCL shipping gives your cargo its own container, available in 20-foot, 40-foot, and 40-foot high-cube (40HQ) sizes. A 40HQ container suits bulkier goods that need more cubic space without adding weight.

 FCL sea freight works well for larger shipments, cargo that needs extra security, or importers looking to lower their per-unit shipping costs. Dedicated container shipping avoids stopping to consolidate goods from other shippers, so FCL shipments tend to clear faster once they reach a US port.

### LCL Shipping and Multi-Supplier Consolidation

 LCL shipping works for importers who don’t have enough cargo to fill a container. We consolidate smaller shipments into shared containers, so you pay for only the space your cargo occupies.

 LCL sea freight also supports multi-supplier consolidation. If a buyer sources from more than one Shenzhen factory, we can combine the shipments into a single consolidated container instead of arranging separate bookings.  Once cargo clears US customs, we can deliver it to a commercial warehouse, retail distribution point, or Amazon fulfillment center. Amazon FBA shipping from China to the USA has its own requirements: FBA labeling, delivery appointment scheduling through Amazon’s Carrier Appointment Request Portal, and sometimes splitting shipments across multiple warehouses.

Handling Amazon FBA shipping from China to USA requires strict adherence to pallet dimensions and appointment windows. ### Comparing Sea Freight to Air and Express Modes

 Evaluating available shipping methods from Shenzhen to USA allows importers to balance speed against landed cost. While ocean shipping provides the lowest landed cost for bulk orders, urgent stock needs may call for faster transport modes.

 Importers sometimes split orders, moving initial cartons via air freight from Shenzhen to USA or using express shipping from Shenzhen to USA for high-priority items. When expedited timelines also require seamless customs payment, air freight DDP delivers cargo quickly with duties prepaid.

 Air cargo departing Shenzhen Bao’an International Airport flies under an air waybill directly into US gateways. For standard inventory replenishment, however, sea freight from Shenzhen to USA remains far more economical than air transport.

 Managing regular shipping from Shenzhen to USA effectively means aligning each production run with the right transport method to balance cash flow and speed.

## How Much Does Door-to-Door Ocean Freight Cost?

 **Door-to-door ocean freight pricing from Shenzhen depends on cargo volume, weight, and how much of the delivery chain you bundle into one quote.** There’s no flat rate for every shipment. A 20-foot FCL container, an LCL pallet, and a shipment headed to an Amazon warehouse all have different cost structures.

### How Cargo Volume, Weight, and Destination Affect Your Quote

 Ocean freight rates are based on CBM (cubic meters) for LCL cargo and container size for FCL. Chargeable weight also matters: carriers charge based on whichever is greater, actual weight or volumetric weight.

 Destination zip code matters too. A shipment headed to a West Coast warehouse typically costs a different amount than one going to an inland address or an East Coast port, because both inland trucking distance and port routing affect the final price.

 Landed cost goes a step further, combining the product price, freight, customs duties, and delivery into one per-unit figure. When requesting a quote, customers can ask for an itemized charge breakdown instead of a single lump-sum number. Customs brokerage, for example, can be included in the quote and usually runs around USD 125, depending on the number of commodities involved. ### DDP vs. Other Shipping Terms: Who Pays Duties and Taxes?

 Choosing DDP shipping from Shenzhen to USA means the freight forwarder arranges payment of import duties and taxes as part of the shipment. This Delivered Duty Paid model ensures the importer doesn’t get an unexpected bill at the door.

 DDP shipping from Shenzhen to the USA and sea freight DDP differ from standard door-to-door service, where the importer remains the Importer of Record and pays duties directly.

 Importers remain responsible for duties and taxes regardless of shipping terms. For Amazon FBA shipments, Amazon doesn’t act as Importer of Record and won’t accept cargo that arrives with unpaid duties, taxes, or shipping charges.

 As a general guide, door-to-door ocean freight from China to the USA takes about 30 to 40 days. This range covers everything from factory pickup to final delivery, not just the port-to-port sailing time, so allow extra time for customs processing and inland trucking.

### Is sea freight priced per kilogram, per CBM, or per container?

 LCL shipping is priced per cubic meter (CBM) because the cargo shares container space with other shipments. FCL is priced per container (20-foot, 40-foot, or 40HQ), so the cost stays flat no matter how full the container is.

### Does door-to-door shipping include customs clearance and import duties?

 Door-to-door shipping includes coordination for customs clearance, but import duties depend on the shipping terms. With DDP shipping, duties are included in the arrangement. Under standard terms, the importer pays duties and taxes directly.
