## Bill of Lading Issuance

After we loaded up and the vessel left port, I issued the House Bill of Lading (HBL).

The bill of lading had all the details:

- Shipper
- Consignee
- Vessel and voyage
- Container number
- Port of loading
- Port of discharge

Leroy asked for a telex release instead of mailing original documents overseas.

Smart choice, honestly—it cuts down on time and risk.

## Telex Release

For this shipment, I used telex release instead of original paper bills of lading.

It lets the consignee pick up the cargo at the destination without waiting for documents to arrive.

Telex release speeds up delivery and skips the headaches of international couriers.

Why make things harder?

## US Entry Filing

For this shipment, **AEB Logistics completed the US Entry filing for the consignee**.

US Entry refers to the customs entry process required by **U.S. Customs and Border Protection** before cargo can be released in the United States.

Our team handled the customs entry preparation, including:

- Commercial Invoice verification
- Packing List review
- HS Code confirmation
- Customs entry submission

By completing the US Entry in advance, the cargo was able to move through the customs clearance process smoothly after arrival.

This service helped the client avoid delays and ensured the shipment was released on time.

## Final Delivery (Proof of Delivery)

### Shipping Terms Explained

There are also different [shipping terms](https://aeblogistics.com/freight-forwarder-ningbo-to-usa/) you might hear about, like [FOB](https://aeblogistics.com/freight-forwarder-shanghai-to-usa/), [CIF](https://aeblogistics.com/freight-forwarder-tianjin-to-usa/), or [DDP](https://aeblogistics.com/ddp-shipping-risk-when-shipping-from-china/).

If these sound confusing, don’t worry—I’ve explained them a hundred times and am happy to do it again.

FOB (Free On Board) means the supplier gets your goods onto the ship, and then you’re in charge.

CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight) includes some extras, but you still have to handle customs on your end.

DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) is the easiest for you, but it can cost a bit more.

Choosing the right shipping term can save you money and headaches.

Sometimes suppliers push for one term because it’s easier for them, but it might not be best for you.

Let’s talk through your options before you decide.

## FAQs

### **What are the key services provided by a freight forwarder for shipping from China to the USA?**

A freight forwarder handles container booking, documentation, insurance, US Entry filing, shipment tracking, telex release, and final delivery to ensure a smooth shipping process.

### **How does the US Entry filing process work and why is it important?**

The US Entry filing involves preparing and submitting necessary customs documents before the shipment arrives in the USA, which helps avoid delays and ensures smooth clearance through US Customs.

### **What is a telex release and how does it benefit the shipping process?**

A telex release allows the consignee to pick up the cargo at the destination without waiting for original bills of lading, speeding up delivery and reducing reliance on international couriers.

### **Why is cargo insurance recommended for shipments from China to the USA?**

Cargo insurance protects against damages, loss at sea, and accidents at ports, providing financial security and peace of mind during transit.

### **What factors should be considered when choosing a port for shipping from China to the USA?**

Considerations include the port’s location, costs, timing, local regulations, and whether it aligns with your delivery schedule and overall logistics strategy.
