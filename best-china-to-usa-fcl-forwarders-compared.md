## Kuehne + Nagel

Kuehne + Nagel is a large global forwarder and suits enterprise importers with multi-country sourcing and structured reporting needs. Its scale brings broad carrier relationships and corporate systems.

## DHL Global Forwarding

DHL Global Forwarding offers ocean freight through a global network that also covers air and contract logistics. Importers who already ship parcels with DHL may appreciate having one vendor.

Request a written scope that separates origin charges, ocean freight, destination handling, and delivery. Large-network quotes can bundle these lines differently from specialist quotes, which makes side-by-side comparisons harder.

## DSV

DSV is a global transport and logistics company with ocean freight, customs, and warehousing services. It suits importers who want one provider to coordinate sea freight with storage and distribution.

Ask how DSV prices drayage and chassis, and whether the quote includes free time at the port. Those two items often explain the gap between a low headline rate and the final invoice.

## Expeditors International

Expeditors International is a US-headquartered global forwarder with customs brokerage and ocean freight capabilities. Mid-size and large importers often consider it when they want strong US customs clearance handling.

Confirm service minimums and account structure early. Providers built for large programs may offer less flexibility on a single container than forwarders that work with smaller accounts.

## Shapiro

Shapiro is one of the premier US-based customs and logistics providers known for guiding US importers through complex regulatory compliance. Its strength lies on the US side of the shipment, coordinating directly with US Customs and Border Protection.

China-origin pricing can differ from pricing by an origin-based forwarder, so ask how the provider manages pickup, export handling, and supplier coordination. Compare the full quote, not just the ocean line.

## Unicargo

Unicargo operates in the US and China and serves e-commerce brands and importers with air and sea options. Its two-sided footprint gives it staff at both ends of the lane.

Check whether your destination is near a port or inland, and who arranges the final delivery appointment. Ask the provider to name the carrier, sailing window, and delivery accessorials in the quote.

## SINO Shipping

SINO Shipping is based in China, Hong Kong, and Taiwan and serves small and mid-sized businesses across air, sea, and rail. As niche FCL route specialists, they focus on China-to-USA ocean freight lanes where origin agility is critical. Being close to the origin can help with factory pickup timing and export documents.

Verify what happens after the container reaches a US port. Ask who files the customs entry, who books the drayage, and how the provider shows destination charges.

## China Freight

China Freight is a Shenzhen-based forwarder that covers FCL and LCL sea freight, air, express, and customs brokerage, with door-to-door delivery from supplier pickup. It targets importers, wholesalers, and Amazon FBA sellers.

For suppliers in Shenzhen and Guangdong, its origin location is a practical advantage. Request an all-in quote that lists origin charges, destination handling, and brokerage separately.

## DocShipper

DocShipper is an international forwarder offering sourcing, warehousing, and shipping services to small and mid-sized importers. Newer importers often consider it when they want guidance as well as transport.

Many cross-border businesses look into DDP shipping options to simplify taxes and delivery. Ask for written inclusions and exclusions covering duties, brokerage, drayage, and detention terms. Compare at least two itemized quotes before booking.

### What Tracking Milestones and Exception Updates Are Available?

Look for named milestones rather than periodic email updates. AEB Logistics tracks Accepted, In transit, Export Customs, Export Departure, Import Entry, Import Arrival, and Delivered, with account manager support for exceptions.

### How Clearly Can Importers Calculate Total Landed Cost?

Landed cost includes product cost, freight, duties, taxes, brokerage, drayage, and delivery, so a quote that stops at ocean freight can’t show the full cost. When providers disclose each line, you can add duties based on your HS code. Treat any quote that leaves out brokerage, drayage, or chassis lines as incomplete.

Check origin pickup, export customs, terminal handling, destination handling, drayage, chassis, and delivery accessorials. Port congestion can lead to extra fees during peak periods. Residential deliveries and pickups can also cost more, so confirm these charges before booking.
