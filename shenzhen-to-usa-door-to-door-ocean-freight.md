## How Much Does Door-to-Door Ocean Freight Cost?

 **A Shenzhen-to-USA ocean freight quote and your actual landed cost are two different numbers. Confusing them is the most common way importers get surprised at delivery.** The freight quote covers transport.

 When calculating ocean freight costs for shipping from Shenzhen to the US, landed cost adds import duties and taxes alongside destination charges.

 Sea freight rates shift with the bunker adjustment factor, port congestion, and demand around peak shipping months. A quote pulled in October can look different by December. That’s normal market behavior, not a sign of a bad forwarder. We recommend requesting a fresh quote close to your cargo-ready date instead of budgeting from an estimate you got months earlier.

 Cargo insurance deserves a line in your request, especially for the electronics and fragile goods Shenzhen factories ship in volume. It costs little compared with freight and duties, and it protects against loss or damage during a transit that can last several weeks.

### What Should a Door-to-Door Freight Quote Include?

 A complete door-to-door freight quote separates five categories: origin pickup, ocean freight, U.S. customs clearance, destination handling, and final-mile delivery. Shipments under DDP shipping terms bundle estimated duties and taxes into the upfront number. With DDU or DAP freight incoterms, the importer pays duties separately at entry.

 We itemize each part rather than handing over one bundled figure. That makes it easy to compare a competing quote line by line and spot what’s missing behind a low headline price, such as destination handling or drayage left off the page.

### How Do FCL, LCL, and Delivery Affect Landed Cost?

 FCL shipping pricing is set per container, so the per-unit cost drops as the box fills. LCL is priced per cubic meter or kilogram, whichever is greater. It costs more per unit, but lets you ship smaller volumes without waiting to fill a container.

 Duties depend on your HS code and declared cargo value, not your freight cost. Two shipments with identical freight quotes can carry very different duty bills if their goods fall under different tariff classifications.

 Final-mile delivery adds another charge. A dock delivery to a business address costs less than a residential drop or a site needing liftgate service. When no loading dock is available, liftgate service carries an additional stated fee of USD 100.

## Which Shenzhen Ocean Service Fits Your Shipment?

 **FCL suits cargo large enough to fill or nearly fill a container, while LCL works for smaller loads that don’t justify booking a whole box.** Both move through the same Shenzhen ports and connect to the same U.S. delivery network. The choice comes down to volume and how much flexibility your deadline allows.

### FCL vs. LCL for Commercial Goods

 With a full container load, your shipment gets its own 20-foot, 40-foot, or 40hq container, sealed from Shenzhen through to U.S. delivery. Standard container shipping ensures nobody else’s cargo shares the box, reducing handling and the risk of damage or mix-ups. We book FCL for larger shipments, cargo that needs extra security, and businesses seeking a lower per-unit cost.

 Less than container load combines your cargo with other freight in a shared container, so you pay only for the cubic meters or kilograms you use. LCL shipping suits new product launches, test orders, and restocks that fall between larger FCL runs. Both options include door-to-door delivery, customs clearance coordination, and warehousing support where needed.

### Factory Pickup and Multi-Supplier Consolidation

 We pick up cargo at the supplier’s factory or warehouse in Shenzhen. Buyers sourcing from multiple Pearl River Delta factories can combine shipments into a single container before departure. Multi-supplier consolidation cuts down on separate bookings and simplifies export filing for Amazon sellers restocking several SKUs from different manufacturers in the same cycle.

### Shenzhen Port Routes and Door-to-Door Transit Planning

 Ocean cargo from Shenzhen mainly moves through Yantian Port, Shekou Port, and Chiwan Port, all of which handle high volumes of electronics and general commercial goods. Port-to-port sea freight takes roughly 14 to 20 days to West Coast gateways like Los Angeles and Long Beach, and about 25 to 32 days to East Coast ports such as Savannah or New York/New Jersey.

 Plan for the full door-to-door chain, including factory pickup, port-to-port transit, import clearance, and final delivery, to take about 30 to 40 days. Customs processing, port congestion, weather, and seasonal demand all affect that window. Building in a buffer before peak season can keep a short delay from turning into a stockout.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does door-to-door ocean freight from Shenzhen to the USA take?

 Plan for about 30 to 40 days door to door, including factory pickup, port-to-port transit, import clearance, and final delivery. Sea freight alone takes roughly 14 to 20 days to West Coast ports and 25 to 32 days to the East Coast. Customs and inland delivery account for the rest of the time.

### Is FCL or LCL cheaper for my Shenzhen shipment?

 FCL costs less per unit once you’ve filled most of a container, since carriers price it per box rather than per cubic meter. LCL costs more per unit, but you don’t have to wait for enough cargo to fill a full container. That usually makes LCL a better fit for smaller shipments or test orders.

### Can I track my shipment after it leaves Shenzhen?

 Yes, you can track each shipment through milestones: Accepted, In Transit, Export Customs, Export Departure, Import Entry, Import Arrival, and Delivered. Your account manager can also provide direct updates alongside the tracking system.

### Who pays U.S. import duties on a door-to-door shipment?

 The importer of record remains responsible for duties and taxes, regardless of the freight incoterms. With DDP shipping, the quote estimates and includes duties upfront. Under DDU or DAP terms, the importer pays separately after the shipment clears customs.

### Can AEB Logistics deliver directly to an Amazon FBA fulfillment center?

 Yes, ocean shipments from Shenzhen can go straight to Amazon FBA fulfillment centers through the same door-to-door booking. Delivery appointments are scheduled through Amazon’s Carrier Appointment Request Portal. Depending on the shipment size, final-mile delivery is handled by LTL or small parcel service.
