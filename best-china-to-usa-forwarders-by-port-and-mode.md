## DHL Global Forwarding: International Air and Ocean Freight

### When Should Tianjin Cargo Depart Locally or Move to Another Gateway?

Tianjin cargo can depart locally when it fills a container or when a direct sailing serves your US port. Smaller loads may move faster if you truck them to a larger gateway, such as Qingdao or Shanghai, where more weekly sailings are available. Ask your forwarder to price both options and specify the extra trucking cost.

### How Should Shenzhen Suppliers Compare Yantian, Shekou, and Air Gateways?

Compare Yantian and Shekou by how often they sail to your US port, then weigh ocean freight against air. Shenzhen Bao’an (SZX) handles air freight, and sailing schedules vary by carrier and terminal. Ask each forwarder which terminal and service it books. Two quotes may use different routes.

### When Does Shanghai Favor Air Freight, LCL, or FCL?

Shanghai is a good fit for air freight when cargo is urgent or high-value, LCL for smaller volumes with flexible dates, and FCL when your volume justifies a dedicated container. With frequent ocean sailings and the PVG air hub, Shanghai offers more options than most origins. Check LCL consolidation cutoffs, which can add days before sailing.

### How Does Your US Delivery Address Affect Gateway Selection?

Your delivery address helps determine the discharge port. West Coast ports serve nearby destinations, while inland and East Coast addresses may require rail or trucking after discharge. A warehouse loading dock can help you avoid liftgate fees. AEB lists a USD 100 liftgate fee when no dock is available, and residential delivery adds accessorial fees. Give each forwarder the exact address.

### When Is Port-to-Port Logistics Enough, and When Is Door-to-Door Better?

Port-to-port works when you already have a customs broker, a bond, and a trucker lined up. Choose door-to-door if you want one company to coordinate factory pickup, clearance, and delivery. Compare the all-in totals, because port-to-port rates leave out the services you’ll need to buy separately.

## How to Compare Quotes and Verify Service Quality

To compare quotes fairly, give every forwarder the same shipment details and make sure each quote lists its exclusions. Put together one request and send it to every provider on your shortlist.

### What Shipment Details Make Competing Quotes Comparable?

Send the same details to each provider: origin and destination, FOB, CIF, or EXW terms, HS code, cargo value, box count, cubic meters, kilograms, and cargo-ready date.

Providing an accurate packing list and commercial invoice upfront prevents miscalculations. AEB notes that accurate addresses, weights, dimensions, and Incoterms help prevent quote discrepancies and unexpected costs. Include a target price, too.

### Which Origin, Destination, and Accessorial Charges Are Included?

Ask providers to list origin pickup, export clearance, ocean or air freight, destination handling, drayage, and delivery as separate line items. Fuel, peak season, and liftgate surcharges often fall outside the base rate. When a quote bundles everything into one number, it’s harder to compare.

### Who Handles Customs Entry, Duties, Bonds, and Importer Responsibilities?

Confirm who files the entry, holds the bond, and pays import duties, including potential Section 301 tariffs on Chinese goods. Ensure the Importer Security Filing (ISF) is submitted timely for ocean cargo to avoid severe penalties.

Small parcel consignments under $800 may leverage the de minimis exemption, but commercial shipments typically require standard entry. Amazon isn’t the Importer of Record, so it won’t accept FBA shipments with unpaid duties, taxes, or shipping charges. If a forwarder clears customs for you, you’ll need to provide a Power of Attorney.

### What Evidence Confirms Route Experience and Delivery Reliability?

Ask how long the provider has served the lane, which origin offices it operates, and which carriers it works with. Then verify the legal entity. AEB, for example, publishes its Chinese entity’s registration details, which you can check through China’s National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System. Ask for references involving similar cargo and destinations.

### How Should You Compare Tracking, Account Support, and Exception Handling?

Check whether you’ll get a named contact, milestone tracking, and a clear process for delays. Verify how quickly you receive transport documents like the ocean bill of lading or air waybill for air shipments.

AEB tracks milestones from Accepted through Export Customs, Export Departure, Import Entry, and Import Arrival to Delivered. Ask each provider how it handles cargo held at customs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which freight forwarder is best for Tianjin, Shenzhen, or Shanghai shipments to the USA?

The best choice depends on your origin office coverage, cargo type, and delivery needs. AEB Logistics suits importers who want a dedicated account manager, itemized quotes, and FBA coordination. Global carriers may be a better fit for multi-country programs. Ask each shortlisted forwarder which local staff cover your supplier’s city.

### What routes do ocean shipments take from China to the USA?

Ocean shipments sail from ports such as Shanghai, Ningbo-Zhoushan, Shenzhen, and Qingdao across the Pacific to terminals like Long Beach and Oakland. Cargo bound for inland addresses continues by rail or truck after discharge. Your forwarder should name the sailing port, discharge port, and inland leg.

### Is using a freight forwarder cheaper than booking transport directly?

A forwarder may lower costs by consolidating cargo and managing documents, but the savings depend on the shipment. LCL pricing lets small importers pay only for the space they use. Compare itemized quotes with the cost of buying those services separately.

### Should I choose air freight, LCL, or FCL for my shipment?

Choose air for urgent or high-value cargo, LCL for smaller volumes with flexible dates, and FCL for larger shipments that justify a dedicated container. AEB says air freight from Hong Kong to Los Angeles takes about 5 days, while ocean takes at least three weeks. Check the total cost, since air is generally more expensive.

### Which forwarders on this list support Amazon FBA delivery?

AEB Logistics and Unicargo specifically target Amazon FBA delivery, and Flexport lists FBA-related services. Before booking, confirm each provider’s labeling, palletization, and appointment-scheduling process. Ask how LCL appointments work once cargo reaches the destination warehouse.

### What is the difference between port-to-port and door-to-door freight forwarding?

Port-to-port covers transport between two ports only. Door-to-door service also includes factory pickup, customs coordination, and final delivery.
