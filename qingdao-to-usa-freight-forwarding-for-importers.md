### How Do CBM, Chargeable Weight, and Delivery Address Affect Price?

Cubic meters and chargeable weight determine how your cargo gets priced, especially for LCL and air freight. Ocean LCL rates are calculated per CBM, while air freight uses chargeable weight, which is the greater of actual weight or volumetric weight. A residential delivery address or a location without a loading dock can also add accessorial charges.

### FOB, EXW, and DDP: Which Charges Are Yours?

Under FOB terms, your Qingdao supplier covers costs until the cargo loads onto the vessel. You cover everything after that, including ocean freight, customs brokerage, and US import duties. Under EXW terms, you or AEB Logistics, acting on your behalf, arrange pickup directly from the factory floor, shifting even more of the process onto your side.

DDP shipping bundles freight, customs clearance coordination, and delivery into one quoted price, making landed costs easier to predict. Importers remain responsible for duties and taxes regardless of the Incoterm. You can also add cargo insurance for protection against loss or damage in transit.

## Which Shipping Mode and US Route Fit Your Cargo?

**FCL suits larger shipments, LCL fits smaller or test orders, and air freight or express courier works for urgent cargo. Routes to Los Angeles, Long Beach, New York, Savannah, or Houston also shape total transit time.** Matching the shipping mode to your cargo size is the biggest way to control both cost and delivery date.

### FCL Ocean Freight for Dedicated Containers

FCL shipping gives your cargo a dedicated container, either 20-foot, 40-foot, or 40-foot high-cube. AEB Logistics handles Qingdao ocean freight bookings, container loading coordination, and door-to-door delivery for shipments large enough to justify a full container. That’s generally when the volume approaches the point where the per-unit shipping cost starts to beat LCL.

### LCL and Consolidated Shipping for Smaller Orders

LCL shipping and consolidated shipping work well for smaller Qingdao orders that don’t fill a container. Your cargo shares container space with other shipments, and you pay only for the space your goods use. AEB Logistics handles the consolidation, customs clearance coordination, and warehousing needed at both ends.

### Air Freight or Express Courier for Urgent Cargo

Air freight from China moves faster than sea freight but costs more per kilogram. A shipment from a China origin point can take around five days to reach a US West Coast gateway like Los Angeles, compared to at least three weeks by ocean. General Amazon FBA air freight lead times run about 7 to 10 days, depending on the shipment and destination. Express courier service suits urgent, small-volume shipments or samples.

### US Gateways and Realistic Door-to-Door Transit Times

Qingdao cargo bound for the US typically routes through West Coast gateways like Los Angeles (LAX for air) or Long Beach, or East Coast ports like New York (JFK for air), Savannah, or Houston. Inland cargo bound for the Midwest sometimes moves through Chicago (ORD) by air or by rail after an ocean leg. General published transit estimates for China-to-USA ocean freight, door to door, run approximately 30 to 40 days. Standard air freight takes approximately 8 to 10 days, while express courier runs approximately 3 to 5 days. Actual times vary with customs processing, port congestion, weather, and seasonal demand.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does freight forwarding from Qingdao to a US delivery address take?

Door-to-door ocean freight generally takes approximately 30 to 40 days. Standard air freight runs approximately 8 to 10 days, while express courier service takes approximately 3 to 5 days. Actual timing depends on customs processing, port congestion, and the final delivery address.

### How much does it cost to ship commercial cargo from Qingdao to the USA?

Costs depend on the shipping mode, container size or CBM, chargeable weight, and delivery requirements. For example, liftgate service adds USD 100 at locations without a loading dock. Customs brokerage support typically costs around USD 125, depending on how many commodities are included in the shipment.

### Should I choose air freight, LCL, or FCL for my import shipment?

Air freight works well for urgent, high-value, lower-volume cargo. LCL suits smaller orders that won’t fill a container, while FCL makes sense for larger shipments that need a full 20-foot, 40-foot, or 40-foot high-cube container. Your deadline, cargo volume, and budget will help you choose.

### What documents does a US importer need before shipping from Qingdao?

US Customs and Border Protection reviews a commercial invoice, packing list, and bill of lading, or an air waybill for air shipments. Make sure the product description, declared value, and consignee details match across all documents to avoid clearance delays.

### Who pays US import duties on a door-to-door shipment?

Importers remain responsible for duties and taxes, even on door-to-door or DDP shipments. For Amazon FBA shipments, Amazon isn’t the Importer of Record and won’t accept cargo that arrives with unpaid duties, taxes, or shipping charges.

### Can AEB Logistics combine suppliers and deliver directly to Amazon FBA?

Yes, you can combine cargo from different Qingdao-area suppliers into one shipment. AEB Logistics handles FBA labeling and inventory management, then schedules the Amazon FBA delivery appointment. If inventory needs to go to multiple fulfillment centers, the team can split the shipment.
